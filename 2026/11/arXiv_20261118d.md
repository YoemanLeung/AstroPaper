## Key Findings

- Resolved gas kinematics reveal disturbed early quasar hosts; a measured velocity gradient alone is insufficient evidence for a rotating disk. ([2611.21755](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21755) Marcel Neeleman).
- Dust-obscured stellar growth can produce plausible progenitors of massive quiescent galaxies, but rare extreme systems cannot explain their full population. ([2611.21761](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21761) Briana Wirag).
- Fourier phases add information to the 21-cm power spectrum in simulations; the forecast improvement still needs tests with foregrounds and calibration errors. ([2611.21760](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21760) Sambit K. Giri).
- Survey-scale fitting can be fast, but uncertainty propagation dominates the quoted production cost and catalog quality must be selected for each scientific use. ([2611.22914](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22914) John Moustakas).

## Early galaxies and black holes

- [2611.21755](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21755) Marcel Neeleman `XQR30: ALMA Observations Reveal the Diverse Structure of Cold Gas within z>6 Quasar Host Galaxies`
  - **Question:** How regularly does cold gas rotate in luminous high-redshift quasar hosts?
  - **Data:**
    - ALMA [C II] and dust imaging of 41 quasars, with 24 newly observed and 17 archival targets; 40 belong to enlarged XQR-30 and one lies at `z=5.783` just below its nominal redshift cut.
  - **Method:**
    - Fit line profiles and thin-disk models, compare kinematic and morphological position angles, and search the quasar-centered spectral windows for companions.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Of 39 [C II] detections, 18 show rotation-dominated kinematics, nine are dispersion-dominated, and 12 lack adequate signal for classification. Eight candidate companions are detected. Dust is more compact than [C II] wherever both sizes are reliable.
  - **Position:** Extends the XQR-30 host census. Velocity gradients do not establish disks; inclination, disk thickness, resolution and non-gravitational motions affect dynamical masses. One or two companion interlopers are expected, and the search does not cover all spectral windows.

- [2611.21761](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21761) Briana Wirag `Diverse Stellar Components Resolved by JWST in the Massive Galaxy SPT0311-58`
  - **Question:** How much stellar growth is hidden in the dusty protocluster core SPT0311-58?
  - **Data:**
    - JWST NIRCam/MIRI imaging, HST photometry, ALMA dust continuum and prior NIRSpec constraints on the clumps of the two central galaxies at `z=6.9`.
  - **Method:**
    - Deblend photometry and fit energy-balanced spectral energy distributions with CIGALE and MAGPHYS; vary star-formation histories and compare with reconstructed histories of later quiescent galaxies.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The western galaxy has stellar mass `2.5 (+3.2/-1.6) x 10^11 Msun` and star-formation rate `1200 (+890/-520) Msun/yr`; the eastern galaxy has `4.9 (+1.8/-0.99) x 10^10 Msun` and `200 (+98/-42) Msun/yr`. The combined system is a plausible progenitor of very massive quiescent galaxies.
  - **Position:** Connects resolved dusty growth to the EXCELS comparison sample. Stellar ages differ by about a factor of three between codes; constant and burst histories fit similarly. Far-infrared coverage is limited, and the rarity of this system prevents it from representing all quiescent-galaxy progenitors.

- [2611.22316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22316) Piero Madau `Lord/Egg of the LRDs: One geometry for its many faces`
  - **Question:** Can one connected geometry explain the continuum, lines and absorption of Lord/Egg?
  - **Data:**
    - The nearby analog at `z=0.1006`: 11 ultraviolet-to-near-infrared photometric bands, infrared emission, published line diagnostics, X-ray weakness and VLT/FORS2 polarization.
  - **Method:**
    - Combine an angle-dependent super-Eddington disk with young stars and dust attenuation; use Cloudy for a stratified broad-line region and compare predicted polarization and absorption.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The continuum fit favors `log(M_BH/Msun)=7.4` and inclination `73 deg`. Stars supply the ultraviolet branch; the reddened disk supplies the optical branch. The geometry also reproduces much of the Balmer-line and polarization behavior.
  - **Position:** A physically connected interpretation of one local analog, not a unique solution for the high-redshift population. Accretion rate, extinction-law slope and stellar age reach grid boundaries; Paschen-line discrepancies and the coupling of calcium heating to grain destruction remain unresolved.

## Gas beyond galaxies

- [2611.22378](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22378) Katlego Morakile `Bent-tailed radio galaxies as tracers of the intrafilament medium`
  - **Question:** Can bent radio galaxies probe gas in cosmic filaments outside rich clusters?
  - **Data:**
    - MeerKAT Galaxy Cluster Legacy Survey imaging toward Abell 22 and optical redshifts identify three bent radio galaxies in structures around `z=0.11-0.13`.
  - **Method:**
    - Infer minimum radio pressure from faint emission, assume ambient pressure equilibrium, and combine jet bending with estimated galaxy velocities in a ram-pressure model.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The inferred density around J0019-26 is approximately `3.8 x 10^-29 g/cm^3`; J0019-25 gives `(0.2-1.1) x 10^-26 g/cm^3`, more consistent with an embedded group. Optical candidate members support the group interpretation.
  - **Position:** Extends radio probes of the warm-hot intergalactic medium. Non-radiating particles, geometry, unresolved jet width and equilibrium assumptions limit the estimates. Sparse redshifts and an isotropic-velocity assumption in anisotropic filaments add substantial uncertainty; X-ray or thermal Sunyaev-Zeldovich confirmation is still needed.

- [2611.21738](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21738) Kung-Yi Su `Shocks and Trails in the Circumgalactic Medium Driven by Runaway Supermassive Black Holes`
  - **Question:** What drives the shock and narrow trail associated with the runaway-black-hole candidate RBH-1?
  - **Data:**
    - Controlled hydrodynamic simulations with a `2 x 10^7 Msun` black hole moving through a uniform gas box; comparison with the observed bow shock and trail at `z=0.96`.
  - **Method:**
    - Vary wind momentum, velocity, time dependence and ambient turbulence; compare simulated shock size and trail morphology with observations.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The successful models require recent wind momentum flux near `6 x 10^32 (n/10^-3 cm^-3) dyn`. A narrow trail in a `10^6 K` medium implies one-dimensional velocity dispersion below roughly `30 km/s` within the tested setup.
  - **Position:** A potential circumgalactic turbulence probe, conditional on the wind interpretation. The models neither resolve the cold emitting phase nor reproduce observed line emission; accretion, magnetic fields, cosmic rays and large-scale density gradients are omitted.

- [2611.21716](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21716) Lisiyuan Yang `A PhEW Good Simulations: I. Galaxy Properties`
  - **Question:** How does explicitly modeling wind exchange with circumgalactic gas change galaxy growth?
  - **Data:**
    - Cosmological GIZMO simulations using the Physically Evolved Winds model, with varying volume and resolution and no active-galactic-nucleus feedback.
  - **Method:**
    - Track exchange of wind mass, metals, momentum and energy; compare stellar masses, star formation, neutral/molecular gas and metallicities with observations and trace gas accretion histories.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Stellar mass functions agree reasonably down to `z~1`; later, galaxies above about `10^11 Msun` overproduce stars. At `z=0`, self-shielded neutral hydrogen masses exceed observations by a factor `1.5-2`, and high-redshift specific star-formation rates remain too low.
  - **Position:** A test of wind-CGM coupling, not evidence that AGN feedback is universally negligible. The proposed late-time heating remedy is illustrated by removing selected stars in post-processing rather than by running a self-consistent AGN model. Ionization treatment and the unresolved star-formation discrepancy qualify the successes.

## Reionization and survey methods

- [2611.21760](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21760) Sambit K. Giri `The phase gradient spectrum: probing the squeezed bispectrum of the 21-cm signal during reionization`
  - **Question:** Can relative Fourier phases recover information lost by the 21-cm power spectrum?
  - **Data:**
    - 21cmFAST and BEoRN reionization simulations with shared initial conditions; mock SKA-Low observations at five redshifts from `6.5` to `10`.
  - **Method:**
    - Measure phase coherence between short-wavelength mode pairs; relate it to the normalized squeezed bispectrum and combine it with the power spectrum in a Fisher forecast including thermal noise and sample covariance.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - For `1000 h`, the combination reduces marginal errors on star-formation efficiency and its mass slope by about `20%`, and on the turnover mass by `7%`. Phase information alone gives broader constraints than the power spectrum.
  - **Position:** A complementary summary statistic, not a demonstrated observational gain. Foregrounds, calibration errors, lightcone effects and non-linear redshift-space distortions are omitted; the intergalactic medium is assumed fully heated. Non-Gaussian likelihoods and parameter degeneracies require fuller inference.

- [2611.22914](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22914) John Moustakas `FastSpecFit: Spectrophotometric Modeling of 38 Million Extragalactic Targets from the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument Second Data Release`
  - **Question:** How can physical properties be estimated consistently across tens of millions of spectra?
  - **Data:**
    - DESI DR2 spectra and broadband photometry for `38,245,183` extragalactic targets; a companion photometry-only catalog uses the same inputs.
  - **Method:**
    - Forward-convolve stellar and emission-line models with each fiber resolution matrix; propagate uncertainty through 50 Monte Carlo refits and compare against independent measurements.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Typical fitting takes about `0.1 s` without Monte Carlo, whereas production processing with 50 realizations has median `4.8 s` per object per core. Use line-amplitude significance for detection and flux inverse variance for subsequent flux analyses.
  - **Position:** A useful survey-scale reference. The manuscript expects public DR2 catalog release in 2027; earlier EDR/DR1 products are distinct. Apply target-specific redshift cuts, require positive velocity-dispersion inverse variance for direct measurements, and avoid continuum-derived physical properties for quasars and broad-line AGN.
