## Executive View

The strongest high-redshift entry is [2607.14355](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14355) Yoshihisa Asada, which characterizes the ultra-faint lensed host of the `z=5.13` type IIP supernova SN Eos. The host has `M_UV=-14.4+/-0.3`, large Lyman-alpha equivalent width, weak [O III], and an inferred metallicity below `1% Z_sun`, making it a direct probe of initial enrichment in a galaxy that would normally sit below blank-field JWST limits.

Gas, environment, and enrichment are the core of the day. [2607.14359](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14359) Glenn G. Kacprzak combines cloud-by-cloud ionization models with galaxy rotation to show that low-ionization CGM clouds near projected major axes are colder, denser, more H I-rich, and about `0.5 dex` more metal poor than comparable clouds at larger azimuthal angles. [2607.14758](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14758) Aaron Myszka uses KCWI observations of two extremely metal-poor galaxies to find high Fe/O at only `~2%` solar oxygen abundance, pointing to rare energetic enrichment channels.

The best methods and survey entries support the profile from lower redshift. [2607.14785](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14785) Ankit Singh removes a major DisPerSE memory bottleneck and builds a `(1 h^-1 Gpc)^3` filament catalogue from `92 million` MDPL2 halos. [2607.14209](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14209) A. Botteon maps Abell 2255 with `336 h` of LOFAR data and traces coherent magnetic-field topology to the virial radius. [2607.14901](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14901) Andrea Gebek introduces a calibration-free COLIBRE-SKIRT pipeline that links cosmological simulations with FUV-to-FIR mock observables using a live dust model.

## High-Redshift Galaxies and Early Enrichment

- [2607.14355](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14355) Yoshihisa Asada et al. `VENUS: an ultra-faint galaxy hosting the metal-poor type II supernova at z=5.13`
  - **Question:** what does the host of a lensed `z=5.13` core-collapse supernova reveal about the first stages of metal enrichment?
  - **Data:**
    - Target: SN Eos and its multiply imaged host galaxy at `z=5.13`.
    - Lensing: total magnification `mu~53`.
    - Host observables: rest-UV magnitude, Lyman-alpha emission, Halpha, and weak [O III].
  - **Method:**
    - Use lensing to spatially localize the supernova within the host.
    - Characterize the local host environment with rest-frame UV and nebular-line constraints.
    - Interpret weak [O III] together with the metal-poor SN as a gas-phase metallicity indicator.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The host is ultra-faint, with `M_UV=-14.4+/-0.3`.
    - The galaxy is a Lyman-alpha emitter with very high equivalent width.
    - [O III] 5007 is weak relative to Hbeta, with `[O III]5007/Hbeta<0.7`.
    - The environment plausibly has metallicity below `1% Z_sun`.
    - Without lensing, the host would be below detection limits in existing NIRCam surveys.
  - **Position:** read first because the source links JWST transient discovery to the otherwise hidden ultra-faint galaxies that may dominate early enrichment.

- [2607.14758](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14758) Aaron Myszka et al. `The Mysterious Case of Iron in XMPs: Anomalous High log(Fe/O) Observed in Extremely Metal-Poor Galaxies HSCJ1631+4426 and SDSSJ0811+4730`
  - **Question:** can local extremely metal-poor galaxies preserve the abundance signatures of rare early energetic explosions?
  - **Data:**
    - Targets: HSCJ1631+4426 and SDSSJ0811+4730.
    - Instrument: Keck Cosmic Web Imager.
    - Wavelength range: `3545-5529 A` with `0.5 A` spectral sampling.
  - **Method:**
    - Measure integrated nebular abundances from KCWI spectra.
    - Use the full IFU field to capture each galaxy.
    - Compare Fe/O and O/H with chemical-evolution expectations.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Oxygen abundances are `12+log(O/H)=7.079+/-0.010` and `6.926+/-0.004`.
    - Fe/O ratios are high, with `log(Fe/O)=-1.57+/-0.17` and `-1.28+/-0.07`.
    - Fe/O approaches or exceeds solar despite oxygen abundance of only `~2%` solar.
    - Core-collapse supernovae alone and delayed Type Ia enrichment do not naturally explain the ratios.
    - Bright hypernovae and/or pair-instability supernovae are plausible enrichment channels.
  - **Position:** useful local analog paper because XMP abundance anomalies can calibrate nucleosynthetic pathways expected in very early galaxies.

- [2607.14237](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14237) Clarke J. Esmerian et al. `Efficient Interstellar Grain Growth from High Sticking Coefficients on Amorphous Carbon Dust`
  - **Question:** can gas-phase accretion grow dust grains efficiently on interstellar timescales?
  - **Data:**
    - Model target: amorphous carbon grain surfaces.
    - Simulations: molecular dynamics impacts by cosmologically abundant gas-phase atoms.
    - Experimental support: laboratory tests on similar dust-candidate materials.
  - **Method:**
    - Simulate atom-grain impacts under realistic ISM gas and grain temperatures.
    - Compute sticking coefficients for non-inert elements.
    - Compare theoretical expectations with experiment.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Non-inert elements show high sticking coefficients, `>=0.2`, across relevant conditions.
    - Small carbon grains can grow on timescales shorter than the age of the Universe.
    - In some ISM phases, growth times approach giant-molecular-cloud lifetimes.
    - Gas-phase accretion can be an efficient dust-mass growth channel.
  - **Position:** relevant to high-redshift dust budgets because rapid in-situ grain growth can reduce tension with early dusty-galaxy observations.

## CGM, Environment, and the Cosmic Web

- [2607.14359](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14359) Glenn G. Kacprzak et al. `The chemodynamical signature of coherent metal-poor inflow and enriched recycled accretion in the cool circumgalactic medium`
  - **Question:** can metallicity and angular momentum together distinguish cool CGM inflow from recycled accretion?
  - **Data:**
    - Sample: `21` galaxies from the Multiphase Galaxy Halos Survey.
    - Measurements: low- and higher-ionization CGM cloud properties.
    - Context: galaxy rotation kinematics and absorber azimuthal angle.
  - **Method:**
    - Model cloud-by-cloud ionization states.
    - Compare metallicity, H I column density, density, temperature, and line broadening with projected azimuthal angle.
    - Test whether clouds are kinematically consistent with disk rotation.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Low-ionization co-rotating major-axis clouds have metallicities lower by `~0.5 dex`.
    - The same clouds have higher `N(H I)`, higher density, and reduced non-thermal broadening.
    - The higher-ionization phase shows no strong metallicity dependence on azimuthal angle.
    - The pattern supports dynamically cold, metal-poor planar inflow plus enriched recycled material at larger azimuthal angles.
  - **Position:** read first for CGM work because it uses metallicity and angular momentum together rather than relying on geometry alone.

- [2607.14785](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14785) Ankit Singh et al. `Enabling Cosmic Web Analysis at Gigaparsec Scales: A Multi Block Approach for DisPerSE`
  - **Question:** how can DisPerSE be scaled to gigaparsec simulations without breaking cosmic-web topology?
  - **Data:**
    - Validation: `300 h^-1 Mpc` MDPL2 subvolume.
    - Full application: `(1 h^-1 Gpc)^3` MDPL2 box.
    - Catalogue: `92 million` halos and connectivity for `22,900` halos.
  - **Method:**
    - Decompose the volume into overlapping blocks.
    - Retain globally valid tetrahedra with a circumsphere criterion.
    - Merge outputs with core filtering, deduplication, and boundary stitching.
    - Compare the result with a monolithic reference calculation.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The validation recovers `99.6%` of total filament length.
    - Density maxima and minima are recovered at `100%`.
    - Individual filament matching reaches `94.7%`.
    - The full-box catalogue confirms the predicted mass-connectivity scaling from groups to clusters.
  - **Position:** important LSS infrastructure because robust filament catalogues are needed for environment, protocluster, and galaxy-gas cross-correlation work.

- [2607.14209](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14209) A. Botteon et al. `The topology of the magnetic field in Abell 2255 out to its virial radius`
  - **Question:** can ultra-deep low-frequency radio data trace cluster magnetic-field topology from core to outskirts?
  - **Data:**
    - Target: Abell 2255 at `z=0.080`.
    - Observations: `336 h` LOFAR Galaxy Cluster Ultra-Deep Field.
    - Imaging: `224 h` best-quality data at `144 MHz`.
  - **Method:**
    - Produce ultra-deep low-frequency images.
    - Apply the synchrotron intensity gradient technique.
    - Compare inferred field orientation with cosmological MHD simulations.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The map reaches `24 microJy beam^-1` sensitivity at `7.1 x 4.3 arcsec` resolution.
    - Magnetic-field orientations vary coherently across halo extensions and relic regions.
    - Cluster dynamics appear to shape the large-scale field out to the virial radius.
    - The result demonstrates a route to SKA-Low cluster-magnetization science.
  - **Position:** useful cluster-environment paper because magnetic topology is part of baryon-cycle and cosmic-web plasma physics.

- [2607.14207](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14207) Roman Akhmetshyn et al. `Measuring satellite galaxy subhalo masses in redMaPPer clusters with UNIONS weak lensing data`
  - **Question:** how strongly are satellite subhalos stripped as they fall into rich clusters?
  - **Data:**
    - Survey: UNIONS weak-lensing data.
    - Cluster sample: redMaPPer clusters.
    - Observable: satellite galaxy-galaxy lensing excess surface density.
  - **Method:**
    - Measure lensing around satellite galaxies at different cluster-centric radii.
    - Fit a simple model for satellite subhalo masses.
    - Compare subhalo-to-stellar mass ratios with cluster-centric position.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The lensing signal has strong statistical power from many lens-source pairs.
    - A simple model reproduces the signal near satellite-centric scales.
    - The model is insufficient at larger scales where the host cluster dominates.
    - Subhalo-to-stellar mass ratio increases with cluster-centric radius.
    - The trend confirms dark-matter stripping or related environmental processing.
  - **Position:** relevant environment paper because subhalo stripping connects cluster dynamics to satellite galaxy evolution.

## AGN Fueling, Dust Models, and Survey Systematics

- [2607.14823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14823) J. L. Tous et al. `Fuelling the central region of galaxies with misaligned gas accretion`
  - **Question:** when does kinematically misaligned gas sustain central star formation and nuclear fueling?
  - **Data:**
    - Survey: MaNGA.
    - Samples: `201` misaligned galaxies and `3689` aligned galaxies.
    - Diagnostics: global sSFR, central concentration, ionized-gas mass, and radial profiles.
  - **Method:**
    - Compare aligned and misaligned systems across stellar mass.
    - Measure central ionized-gas concentration and star-formation profiles.
    - Separate nuclear-activity and non-activity cases.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Misaligned gas produces more centrally concentrated ionized gas than aligned gas.
    - The strongest central gas enhancement occurs at `10^10-10^10.6 M_sun`.
    - Below `10^10 M_sun`, misaligned gas can fuel nuclear star formation at main-sequence levels.
    - At higher masses, the larger pre-existing stellar mass dilutes rejuvenation.
    - Misaligned galaxies with and without nuclear activity have similar central gas concentrations.
  - **Position:** useful AGN-fueling context because external gas supply can create a central reservoir even when duty cycles are short.

- [2607.14901](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14901) Andrea Gebek et al. `The COLIBRE-SKIRT pipeline: Calibration-free dust radiative transfer postprocessing for cosmological simulations`
  - **Question:** can live-dust cosmological simulations be converted into multiwavelength mock observables without calibrated attenuation fixes?
  - **Data:**
    - Simulation suite: COLIBRE.
    - Radiative-transfer tool: SKIRT.
    - Observable target: FUV-to-FIR spatially integrated spectra and the local cosmic spectral energy distribution.
  - **Method:**
    - Use simulated dust masses and species fractions directly.
    - Add no birth-cloud attenuation model in postprocessing.
    - Map two-size multi-species dust distributions into continuous grain-size distributions with a split-and-scale approach.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - A large-volume cosmological simulation reproduces the local Universe CSED without a priori postprocessing calibration.
    - Residual mid-infrared tension is about `0.2 dex`.
    - The tension points to hot-dust heating and PAH-emission carrier uncertainties.
    - The framework can generate synthetic spectra and images from the FUV to FIR at low and high redshift.
  - **Position:** important for survey interpretation because dust postprocessing controls synthetic JWST, ALMA, and rest-frame UV-to-FIR comparisons.

- [2607.14971](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14971) Morgan Micharski and Chris Richardson `The Goldilocks Molecule: H2 Emission Lines Can Identify Elusive Dwarf AGN`
  - **Question:** can near-infrared H2 emission ratios reveal dwarf AGN when optical and coronal-line diagnostics fail?
  - **Data:**
    - Models: photoionization models with active intermediate-mass black holes.
    - Template: an observed WISE dwarf AGN.
    - Diagnostic lines: H2 near-infrared transitions and Brgamma.
  - **Method:**
    - Model emission from dwarf galaxies hosting intermediate-mass black holes.
    - Test H2-to-Brgamma and H2 line-ratio diagnostics.
    - Identify density and black-hole-mass regimes where active IMBHs remain visible.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - H2 2.12 micron to Brgamma can misclassify many dwarf AGN as star forming.
    - Ratios between H2 emission lines can reveal active IMBHs at `log n_H>=4.0`.
    - The method remains promising for `M_BH=10^3 M_sun`.
    - H2 diagnostics may recover systems where optical strong lines and NIR coronal lines are undetected.
  - **Position:** relevant black-hole demographics paper because low-mass AGN selection affects seed and occupation constraints.
