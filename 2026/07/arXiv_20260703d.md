## High-Redshift Selection and Early AGN Infrastructure

- [2607.02353](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02353) Onnalin Innala et al. `Assessing Ultra-Cool Dwarf Contamination in Photometrically Selected High-Redshift Galaxy Samples`
  - **Question:** how many ultra-cool dwarfs contaminate photometric high-redshift galaxy samples?
  - **Data:**
    - Targets: synthetic T- to early Y-type ultra-cool dwarfs compared with photometric high-z galaxy selections.
    - Surveys: HST COSMOS, Roman deep-tier survey configurations, and JWST-like deep surveys.
    - Models: ELF OWL and BOBCAT ultra-cool-dwarf spectral libraries plus stellar number-density prescriptions.
  - **Method:**
    - Build FC-ENZO, a survey-dependent foreground-contamination estimator.
    - Simulate dwarf-star colors and apply user-defined high-redshift selection functions.
    - Compare contaminant fractions as a function of magnitude, metallicity, spectral library, and survey depth.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The dominant contaminants are T to early Y dwarfs most often misclassified as galaxies near `z~8`.
    - ELF OWL predicts larger contaminant fractions than BOBCAT because it has stronger absorption around `1 micron`.
    - Contamination concentrates near each survey limiting magnitude.
    - At brighter magnitudes, relative contamination is highest for HST COSMOS, then Roman deep, then JWST.
  - **Position:** read first because high-z luminosity functions, spectroscopic follow-up queues, and Roman field design all depend on foreground rejection.

- [2607.02366](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02366) Jose Afonso et al. `The Road to Identifying the Earliest Radio-Powerful AGN with the SKA`
  - **Question:** how can SKA surveys find the missing population of radio-powerful AGN at `z>6`?
  - **Data:**
    - Population: currently known and predicted radio-powerful AGN during the Epoch of Reionization.
    - Facilities: SKAO and pathfinder radio surveys.
    - Frequency range: SKAO coverage from `50 MHz` to `15 GHz`.
  - **Method:**
    - Synthesize hydrodynamical and semi-analytic predictions with existing observational constraints.
    - Evaluate selection biases tied to sparse radio spectral information.
    - Propose broadband radio SED, spectral-curvature, jet-evolution, and radio-only redshift diagnostics.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Only a few radio-powerful AGN are known at `z>6`, far below many theoretical expectations.
    - Current incompleteness likely reflects both intrinsic rarity and radio-selection limitations.
    - Fine frequency sampling with SKAO can identify high-z AGN without relying only on optical or near-infrared selection.
    - The same population can become a 21 cm absorption probe of early neutral hydrogen.
  - **Position:** important infrastructure paper because early SMBH demographics need radio selection that is not tied to rest-UV visibility.

- [2607.01330](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01330) Guozhen Ma et al. `Extreme outflow velocities and weak UV emission lines indicate quasars shedding their dust cocoons`
  - **Question:** what physical state produces LoBAL quasars with extreme outflows and weak rest-UV emission lines?
  - **Data:**
    - Sample: six quasars similar to GQ 1309+2904.
    - Redshifts: systemic values `z~2.07-3.28` from detected Halpha emission.
    - Observables: BAL troughs, broad-band `BVR` polarimetry, continuum slopes, and Halpha-based black-hole masses.
  - **Method:**
    - Measure BAL velocities and classify LoBAL systems.
    - Fit reddened quasar continua with SMC-like and steeper extinction laws.
    - Use polarimetry to test inclination and scattering interpretations.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - All targets are quasars with highly blueshifted BALs and outflow velocities up to `~0.16c`.
    - Five targets are confidently identified as LoBAL quasars.
    - Black-hole masses are `10^8.7-10^9.4 M_sun`, with Eddington ratios `0.14-0.34`.
    - The objects are consistent with weak-line quasars seen through a dust-processing disc wind.
  - **Position:** useful for quasar-evolution work because it ties BAL geometry, dust processing, and weak-line behavior to an emergence phase.

- [2607.01479](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01479) Mohammad Hassan Naddaf `Radiative filtering unifies broad-line phenomenology in active galactic nuclei`
  - **Question:** why do broad emission lines weaken or disappear in both low- and high-accretion AGN?
  - **Data:**
    - Systems: low-luminosity AGN, LINERs, weak-line quasars, and high-accretion AGN.
    - Diagnostics: broad emission line strength, Baldwin effect, and `R_Fe` behavior.
    - Model inputs: intrinsic ionizing capability and transmission through filtering gas.
  - **Method:**
    - Model the line-forming ionizing field as intrinsic ionizing power multiplied by effective transmission.
    - Let intrinsic ionizing capability rise with accretion rate while transmission declines at high accretion.
    - Compare the resulting finite line-formation window with broad-line phenomenology.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Broad-line production occupies a non-universal accretion window.
    - Weak or absent broad lines at low and high accretion rates can share one filtering mechanism.
    - Standard BLR scaling relations should break down in extreme accretion regimes.
  - **Position:** relevant to LRD and weak-line-quasar interpretation because broad-line visibility may trace filtering rather than only black-hole mass or luminosity.

## Gas, Environment, and Survey Diagnostics

- [2607.02162](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02162) M. Ramatsoku et al. `Resolved HI and Environmental Dynamics`
  - **Question:** what will SKA-era resolved HI add to environmental galaxy-evolution studies?
  - **Data:**
    - Precursors: MeerKAT, FAST, and ASKAP resolved HI observations.
    - Environments: Virgo, Fornax, nearby filaments, groups, and isolated galaxies.
    - Future facility: SKA-mid AA4 deep, contiguous HI imaging.
  - **Method:**
    - Review observed HI tails, stripping signatures, accretion signatures, and replenishment events.
    - Compare cluster and low-density environments where ram pressure and tidal effects can mix.
    - Forecast SKA-mid AA4 sensitivity and physical resolution.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Resolved HI already reveals ram pressure at unexpectedly large cluster-centric distances.
    - Fornax and filament systems show mixed tidal and hydrodynamical gas processing.
    - SKA-mid AA4 can reach column-density sensitivities of `10^18-10^19 cm^-2` at `~10` to `1-2 kpc` physical resolution.
    - Wide-area resolved HI samples will connect local environmental physics to statistically representative galaxy evolution.
  - **Position:** useful CGM and environment roadmap because it turns SKA survey design into measurable gas-processing channels.

- [2607.02342](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02342) S. Ranchod et al. `The VLA-COSMOS 3 GHz Large Project: Polarised source counts and catalogue`
  - **Question:** what polarized source population appears in the deepest 3 GHz VLA-COSMOS polarization data?
  - **Data:**
    - Survey: VLA-COSMOS 3 GHz Large Project.
    - Resolution: `1.5 arcsec`.
    - Sensitivity: `2.6 uJy/beam` in Faraday depth.
  - **Method:**
    - Process Stokes Q and U mosaicked channel maps.
    - Select total-intensity sources and run 3D rotation-measure synthesis.
    - Define an empirical polarized-emission threshold and construct source counts.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The catalog contains `65` polarized sources, or `51 deg^-2`, above threshold.
    - The 3 GHz cumulative and Euclidean-normalized counts are consistent with 1.4 GHz literature results.
    - No star-forming galaxies are detected in polarization.
    - The `2 sigma` upper limit on polarized star-forming-galaxy density is `<2.0 deg^-2`.
  - **Position:** practical COSMOS radio reference because faint polarized source counts set expectations for SKA magnetic-field and star-forming-galaxy work.

- [2607.01832](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01832) Junjie Huang et al. `Post-starburst Galaxies with Active Galactic Nucleus: Properties and Evolutionary Sequences`
  - **Question:** how do post-starburst regions with AGN activity fit into quenching pathways?
  - **Data:**
    - Survey: SDSS-IV MaNGA final data release.
    - Samples: `48` AGN-PSBs, `92` central PSBs, `89` ring-like PSBs, and `828` irregular PSBs.
    - Diagnostics: Balmer absorption, nebular emission, spatially resolved `D_n4000`, kinematics, and merger indicators.
  - **Method:**
    - Identify regions with simultaneous PSB and nuclear-activity signatures.
    - Compare AGN-PSBs with central, ring-like, irregular, and control systems.
    - Use radial age and `V_star/sigma_star` profiles to infer evolutionary links.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - AGN-PSBs have younger central stellar populations than outskirts, like central and ring-like PSBs.
    - High-mass central PSBs show the highest merger-remnant and gas-star misalignment fractions.
    - AGN-PSBs and ring-like PSBs have lower and comparable merger-remnant fractions.
    - Ring-like PSBs may evolve into AGN-PSBs, while high-mass central PSBs likely follow a different path.
  - **Position:** useful low-redshift analog paper because it separates AGN coincidence from merger-driven quenching requirements.

- [2607.02351](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02351) Irina Acharova et al. `Indicatives of Early Stages of Star Formation in the Universe`
  - **Question:** can globular-cluster metallicity structure be connected to circumgalactic cloud enrichment?
  - **Data:**
    - Comparisons: metallicity distributions of globular clusters and circumgalactic clouds.
    - Redshift span: `0.2-5.9`.
    - Model cloud: `10^8 M_sun` circumgalactic cloud with sequential enrichment.
  - **Method:**
    - Compare distribution maxima at `[X/H]~-2.6, -2.0, -1.4, -0.5`.
    - Calculate staged enrichment by successive globular-cluster formation events.
    - Test whether fixed stellar mass formation per stage can reproduce the observed structure.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The two metallicity distributions share a sequence of local maxima.
    - Formation of one or more clusters totaling `3 x 10^6 M_sun` per enrichment stage can reproduce the pattern.
    - The maximum stellar mass capable of producing supernovae increases with metallicity in the model.
  - **Position:** secondary for this profile, but relevant where globular-cluster formation is treated as a fossil record of CGM enrichment.

- [2607.02480](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02480) C. Tsakonas et al. `The Merger-Driven Origin of the Vast Extended Stellar Disc Around the Andromeda Galaxy`
  - **Question:** can M31's extended stellar disc be explained by a recent major merger?
  - **Data:**
    - Target: M31's remote disc, inner-halo substructures, and two compact outer-disc globular-cluster groups.
    - Simulation: an N-body hydrodynamical major-merger analog.
    - Merger parameters: gas-rich major event with mass ratio `1:4` about `2-4 Gyr` ago.
  - **Method:**
    - Compare model particles with resolved tracers in the M31 disc.
    - Track pre- and post-merger disc structure, stretching, heating, and warping.
    - Compare stellar rotation with HI gas and globular-cluster structure.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The merger transforms the progenitor disc into a kinematically hot and asymmetric structure.
    - The post-merger disc is stretched by nearly a factor of `2` and reaches beyond `40 kpc`.
    - The old stellar warp has inclination decreasing with radius.
  - **Position:** lower-redshift context paper for interpreting extended discs and merger-built outskirts in massive galaxies.
