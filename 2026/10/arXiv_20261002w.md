## Key Findings

- The week's CGM studies probe different density weightings. [2609.30834](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30834) Evan Haze Nunez-Cravin resolves ionized absorption around low-mass galaxies, while [2610.01454](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01454) Andrea Travascio and [2610.01400](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01400) Titouan Lazeyras require dense emission-weighted structure around quasars. They support heterogeneous gas without determining one universal halo density PDF.
- Galaxy activity is an imperfect map of environment. [2610.01136](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01136) Canze Deng and [2609.35987](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35987) Siwei Zou find selection-dependent dusty-galaxy associations; [2609.37445](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37445) Sofia G. Gallego locates bright gas away from the quasar; [2610.01816](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01816) Jun Toshikawa finds diversity hidden by pooling protoclusters.
- Rare sightlines and population averages give complementary reionization tests. [2609.38080](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38080) Jakob M. Helton and [2609.36656](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36656) Minghao Yue analyze the same transparent sightline, with different environmental selections. [2609.35221](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35221) Shunta Shimizu remains tentative, and [2609.40363](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40363) Aritra Kundu derives neutral fractions conditional on intrinsic Lyalpha emission and bubble morphology.
- Early enrichment is measurable, but calibration and selection still shape the answer. [2609.34827](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34827) L. Parente infers an unobserved strong oxygen line; [2610.01811](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01811) C. Moya-Sierralta contrasts LAE and mass-selected baselines; [2609.35471](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35471) Yoshihiro Takeda finds only marginal environmental offsets. These results do not establish a universal protocluster metallicity shift.
- Candidate physics remains distinct from demonstrated mechanisms. [2609.35945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35945) J. Scholtz does not confirm Pop III stars; [2609.31106](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31106) Sharon Lapiner forecasts a cloudlet test; [2609.40233](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40233) C. Cornil-Baiotto resolves tracer decorrelation in one galaxy without establishing a universal cloud lifetime.

## CGM structure from absorption, emission, and propagation

- [2610.01454](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01454) Andrea Travascio et al. `Constraining the density distribution of the cold Circumgalactic Medium of quasars at z>2 through Lya, HeII and Ha emission`
  - **Question:** What density structure produces weak HeII relative to Halpha around luminous quasars?
  - **Data:**
    - KCWI observations of 13 quasars at `z~2.2` with targeted MOSFIRE follow-up, plus 26 deep MUSE quasar fields at `3.1<z<4.5`.
  - **Method:**
    - Measure lines in matched apertures and randomized spectral stacks; compare with CLOUDY single-density and truncated lognormal cloud models.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Within selected slit regions out to `35 pkpc`, stacked `HeII/Halpha=0.54 (+0.32/-0.21)` and `Lyalpha/Halpha=9.05 (+5.30/-3.24)` favor recombination from an unresolved dense contribution.
    - For the fiducial EUV slope and median densities `10^-3-10^-1 cm^-3`, models matching the median ratio have finite-grid `C~700-4000`. This is not a unique halo-wide clumping measurement: PDF cutoff, illumination, and bright-region selection matter.
  - **Position:** Extends non-resonant CGM diagnostics from individual nebulae to a sample with matched hydrogen and helium measurements.

- [2610.01400](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01400) Titouan Lazeyras et al. `Resolving the physics of Quasar Lyalpha Nebulae (RePhyNe): II. The dense and clumpy CGM of Quasars at z ~ 3.5`
  - **Question:** Do simulated quasar halos reproduce the distribution of bright circumgalactic emission?
  - **Data:**
    - DaLya zoom simulations at two resolutions, COLIBRE m5/m6 and HELLO, compared with MUSE quasar nebulae at `z~3.8`.
  - **Method:**
    - Construct maximal-fluorescence mock maps and compare pixel brightness distributions, cold-gas density PDFs and predicted HeII/Lyalpha ratios.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Most runs underproduce the covering fraction of bright emission; COLIBRE m5 is an exception. The brightness comparison requires gas reaching at least `10 cm^-3` within `50-200 ckpc` under the adopted emission model.
    - At fixed median density, the line-ratio constraint requires clumping factors at least `8` times those of the simulated PDFs. Resolution, self-shielding and cooling are coupled; no unique missing mechanism is established.
  - **Position:** Shows why a mean radial brightness profile can conceal failures in the spatial distribution of emitting gas.

- [2609.30834](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30834) Evan Haze Nunez-Cravin et al. `KBSS-InCLOSE II: First Detailed Insights on the Inner CGM of Low-Mass z~2.3 Galaxies`
  - **Question:** What does the inner CGM of low-mass cosmic-noon galaxies contain?
  - **Data:**
    - Two galaxies at `z~2.245`, within `50 kpc` of a quasar sightline; KCWI/MOSFIRE emission, HIRES absorption and imaging.
  - **Method:**
    - Combine systemic redshifts and galaxy sizes with Voigt-profile decomposition and thermal/turbulent linewidth separation.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The CGM has weak HI, intermediate/high ions and at least `7` velocity components; the typical inferred temperature is `10^4 K`.
    - Neither galaxy has unambiguously unbound detected gas. Two sightlines and projection prevent a population-wide outflow census.
  - **Position:** An absorption-selected counterpoint to bright quasar-nebula emission, sampling a different gas weighting.

- [2609.31106](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31106) Sharon Lapiner et al. `Probing the cosmic web with fast radio bursts: II. Scintillation`
  - **Question:** Can FRB scintillation probe cold cloudlets in intervening halos and filaments?
  - **Data:**
    - Analytic multiphase cosmic-web models and population predictions for FRB sightlines; no new cloudlet detection.
  - **Method:**
    - Combine cooling-length cloudlets, turbulent scattering screens, screen geometry and intercept probabilities.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - In the fiducial model, many bursts with extragalactic `DM>500 pc cm^-3` lose Galactic scintillation below about `1 GHz` if halo cloudlets have filling factors above about `10^-4`.
    - Cloud sizes, non-thermal pressure, turbulent damping and multiple screens change the signal. Present scintillation samples are too small for this proposed population test.
  - **Position:** Links unresolved gas structure to radio propagation independently of nebular-emission diagnostics.

- [2609.37445](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37445) Sofia G. Gallego et al. `Caught in the act: diffuse stellar and Lyalpha emission around a halo-less quasar in the assembling cluster CARLA J0800+4029 at z=2`
  - **Question:** Where is the observable cool gas in a disturbed quasar-hosting cluster?
  - **Data:**
    - KCWI spectroscopy and HST imaging of CARLA J0800+4029 at `z~1.986`.
  - **Method:**
    - Detect connected Lyalpha emission and measure its raw-cube flux; compare a quasar-centered limit with matched area and spectral width.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The offset complex has `L_Lyalpha=1.42e43 erg/s`; the matched nuclear-halo `3-sigma` limit is `4.20e42 erg/s`.
    - Tidal redistribution, illumination, dust and radiative transfer can contribute. The spatial offset does not uniquely demonstrate AGN removal of gas.
  - **Position:** Shows why the position of an active nucleus need not locate the brightest diffuse gas.

## Environmental diversity and early chemical enrichment

- [2610.01816](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01816) Jun Toshikawa et al. `Diverse Protoclusters at z~3 Disclosed by Systematic Spectroscopy`
  - **Question:** Can comparable selection reveal diversity among protoclusters at cosmic noon?
  - **Data:**
    - HSC-SSP/CLAUDS U-dropout overdensities, Subaru/FOCAS spectroscopy of 341 targets and multiband SEDs in three fields.
  - **Method:**
    - Test redshift peaks against randomized selection functions; compare Lyalpha EW at fixed UV luminosity and fit stellar populations with CIGALE.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Structures at `z~3.05, 3.07, 3.09` contain at least `6, 9, 10` confirmed members. ID5 has enhanced Lyalpha EW, whereas the pooled protocluster sample is field-consistent.
    - Different assembly stages or HI transmission could explain the diversity, but HI is not directly measured. Sparse Lyalpha-selected spectroscopy and incomplete spatial coverage limit quenching and descendant-mass claims.
  - **Position:** Connects systematic overdensity selection to the field-to-field variation hidden by mean environmental trends.

- [2610.01136](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01136) Canze Deng et al. `Do Submillimeter Galaxies Trace Megaparsec Large-scale Structures? -- An Overdensity Analysis of 449 Submillimeter Galaxies in COSMOS`
  - **Question:** How reliably do submillimeter galaxies identify megaparsec-scale overdensities?
  - **Data:**
    - 449 A3COSMOS SMGs, including 168 spectroscopic redshifts and 52 CO/[CII] redshifts, with COSMOS2025 galaxies and matched main-sequence controls.
  - **Method:**
    - Apply the Poisson Probability Method in five association categories; compare mass/redshift-matched controls and inspect morphology and redshift-selection effects.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Across increasingly permissive criteria, association fractions span `11.5-71.2%` for the CO/[CII] subset and about `1.3-61%` for all SMGs. An SMG is therefore a probabilistic signpost, not a guaranteed protocluster member.
    - The main catalog discussion reports `26` new robust candidates, while the final summary paragraph says `31`; this internal inconsistency needs clarification. Reported excess significances use the scatter of five control resamplings and should not be read as fully calibrated Gaussian discovery significances.
  - **Position:** Provides a broad environmental catalog while making redshift quality and tracer selection central to interpretation.

- [2609.35987](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35987) Siwei Zou et al. `COSMOS-3D: Diverse Environments and Hot-dust Signatures among Dusty Star-forming Galaxies at z = 4.9-7.2`
  - **Question:** Do dusty galaxies at `z~5-7` occupy the same density peaks?
  - **Data:**
    - 18 COSMOS-3D DSFGs/candidates with grism spectra, heterogeneous ALMA coverage, MIRI imaging and photometric-redshift PDFs.
  - **Method:**
    - Compare line-emitter counts and PDF-weighted neighbors; separate merger and MIRI-selected subsamples.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The four merging DSFGs have the strongest compact companion excess; five MIRI-detected systems do not share the same pattern.
    - This associates morphology and selection with environment, but does not establish an evolutionary sequence. Small heterogeneous subsets and incomplete line selection remain important.
  - **Position:** Complements the larger COSMOS SMG catalog with higher-redshift, tracer-dependent environmental structure.

- [2610.01811](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01811) C. Moya-Sierralta et al. `Galaxy Protoclusters as Drivers of Cosmic Reionization: II. Te-Based Metallicities of Lyman-alpha Emitters`
  - **Question:** Are LAEs in a reionization-era protocluster chemically distinct from field galaxies?
  - **Data:**
    - Six LAEs with usable JWST/NIRSpec G395H coverage in LAGER-z7OD1 at `z~6.93`; NIRCam and UltraVISTA photometry.
  - **Method:**
    - Measure [OIII]4363 temperatures and O+/O++ abundances at adopted `n_e=300 cm^-3`; propagate Balmer-decrement uncertainty and fit BEAGLE stellar masses.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Five auroral detections span `12+log(O/H)~7.2-8.0`; a five-object stack gives `7.76 (+0.06/-0.05)`, about `0.26 dex` above one comparison LAE stack.
    - The sample lies below several mass-metallicity baselines, but a small LAE-selected sample, uncertain masses and different diagnostics prevent a unique environmental interpretation. Pristine inflow is a proposed explanation, not a measured gas flow.
  - **Position:** Adds direct-method chemical constraints in a known early overdensity without establishing a universal enrichment offset.

- [2609.35471](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35471) Yoshihiro Takeda et al. `Diverse Metallicities in Protoclusters at z ~ 3.4 detected by Deep Learning`
  - **Question:** Do machine-learning-selected protoclusters share chemical enrichment relative to the field?
  - **Data:**
    - Subaru/MOIRCS follow-up of four PCFNet fields, yielding 57 [OIII] emitters and four structures at `z=3.316-3.425`.
  - **Method:**
    - Confirm redshift peaks, fit stellar populations and compare stacked [OIII]/Hbeta metallicities on an adopted high-metallicity branch.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Two structures have field-relative offsets at only `1.4-2 sigma`; the other two are consistent with the field.
    - Ionization conditions, possible weak AGN and stellar-mass uncertainty qualify the abundance comparison. Gas recycling versus dilution remains an interpretation.
  - **Position:** A useful comparison for the heterogeneous environmental chemistry seen in reionization-era LAEs.

- [2609.34827](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34827) L. Parente et al. `DREAMS: Taking the Temperature of a Massive Galaxy at z ~ 11`
  - **Question:** How enriched is a compact galaxy within the first few hundred million years?
  - **Data:**
    - JWST/NIRSpec medium-resolution spectra and NIRCam photometry of mildly lensed Janus-11; [OIII]4363 is detected at `S/N=8.5`.
  - **Method:**
    - Infer unobserved [OIII]5007 from Ne3O2, adopt density and nebular-reddening prescriptions, and derive temperature and O/H.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The inferred `T_e=21000 +/- 2000 K` gives `12+log(O/H)=7.74 (+0.11/-0.10)`.
    - This is auroral-line-based but not a fully measured 4363/5007 ratio. Reddening, the line-proxy calibration and a possible AGN contribution limit interpretation.
  - **Position:** Pushes chemical measurements to very high redshift while exposing a critical inferred-line step.

## Reionization, transparent sightlines, and primordial candidates

- [2609.35221](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35221) Shunta Shimizu et al. `Probing Spatial Variations in IGM Transmission: Higher Lyalpha Visibility in an Halpha-Selected Galaxy Overdensity at z~eq6.2`
  - **Question:** Is Lyalpha more visible inside an independently selected overdensity at `z~6.2`?
  - **Data:**
    - JWST F470N Halpha and HSC NB872 Lyalpha imaging in CEERS; 21 HAEs in the matched-UV comparison.
  - **Method:**
    - Compare inner/outer LAE fractions with photometric Monte Carlo and finite-sample binomial uncertainty; test galaxy-property trends.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The inner and outer LAE fractions are `0.41 (+0.15/-0.14)` and `0.27 (+0.19/-0.15)`; the exact-test probability is about `0.38`.
    - This is tentative evidence, not a confirmed ionized bubble. A narrower Lyalpha filter and unknown line-of-sight structure can mimic part of the contrast.
  - **Position:** Uses a non-Lyalpha environmental selection to probe patchy transmission near reionization completion.

- [2609.38080](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38080) Jakob M. Helton et al. `Discovery of the Lyman-alpha Forest in a Star-Forming Galaxy at z approximately 7.57: Implications for the Timing and Topology of Cosmic Reionization`
  - **Question:** Can flux blueward of a galaxy Lyalpha break reveal a transparent reionization-era sightline?
  - **Data:**
    - Deep JADES NIRSpec spectroscopy and NIRCam imaging of JADES-GS-z7-LAF.
  - **Method:**
    - Check spectral and imaging centroids, line-spread-function leakage and interlopers; compare transmission with foreground structures and simulations.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Blueward flux is detected in spectroscopy and imaging; the analysis associates transmission windows with several foreground overdensities.
    - It is one sightline shared with the companion analysis. Unresolved transmission structure, continuum modeling and imperfectly excluded foreground contamination limit topology and ionizing-escape claims.
  - **Position:** Extends Lyalpha-forest work to a star-forming galaxy without measuring the global ionization fraction directly.

- [2609.36656](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36656) Minghao Yue et al. `An Ionized Superstructure at Cosmic Dawn Revealed by a Foundation Model for Astrophysical Research`
  - **Question:** What physical interpretation follows from the broad blueward transmission feature?
  - **Data:**
    - JADES-GS-217704, the same `z=7.567` source as JADES-GS-z7-LAF, and depth-limited JADES photometry.
  - **Method:**
    - Fit a Gaussian transmission window and continuum; compare simulated skewers and flux-limited photo-z neighbor counts.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The fitted window corresponds to `210.5 (+55.7/-53.5) cMpc` centered at `z=6.80 +/- 0.07`; this is a model-dependent line-of-sight scale.
    - The supplementary experiment-level probability reaches `0.005`, qualifying the stronger headline tension. No strong excess in the selected bright-neighbor sample does not exclude faint overdensities or settle inside-out versus outside-in reionization.
  - **Position:** A complementary analysis of the same observation, not an independent second transparent sightline.

- [2609.40363](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40363) Aritra Kundu et al. `Probing the Reionization History and Bubble Sizes with JWST Lyman-alpha Fraction Measurements`
  - **Question:** What reionization history follows from UV-selected Lyalpha fractions?
  - **Data:**
    - Published Lyalpha fractions at `z~6-14` and the `95.5 cMpc` THESAN-1 simulation.
  - **Method:**
    - Calibrate intrinsic EW distributions at `z~6`; compute damping-wing transmission and vary neutral fraction with bubble sizes fixed at matched neutral fraction.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - For `EW>25 Angstrom`, the inferred mass-weighted neutral fractions are `0.38 (+0.14/-0.38)`, `0.57 (+0.23/-0.57)` and `0.93 (+0.05/-0.17)` at `z~7, 8, 11`.
    - Intrinsic EW evolution and ISM/CGM absorption can shift these values. Agreement in population statistics does not by itself explain the rare JADES sightline.
  - **Position:** Supplies a population-level comparison to the week's individual transmission and environmental measurements.

- [2609.35945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35945) J. Scholtz et al. `GA-NIFS view of LAP1: Assembly of Pop III stellar clusters during EoR`
  - **Question:** Do the lensed LAP1 components require primordial ionizing populations?
  - **Data:**
    - JWST NIRSpec IFS and MSA spectra plus stacked NIRCam imaging of LAP1-A/B.
  - **Method:**
    - Combine line diagnostics and lensing constraints; bootstrap the tentative CIV signal and compare independent reductions.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Adopted calibrations give `12+log(O/H)=6.8 +/- 0.2` and `6.5 +/- 0.2` for A/B; CIV significance is only `2.1-2.6 sigma`.
    - Pop II and Pop III enrichment remain allowed. Undetected continuum, magnification, abundance calibration and size assumptions qualify stellar and dynamical mass limits.
  - **Position:** A prime low-metallicity follow-up target that remains a candidate rather than a primordial-star confirmation.

## Resolved gas and star formation

- [2609.40233](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40233) C. Cornil-Baiotto et al. `Sub-kiloparsec Test of the Kennicutt-Schmidt Relation in a Strongly Lensed Dusty Star-Forming Galaxy at z~2.78`
  - **Question:** At what scale does a resolved gas-star-formation relation cease to describe individual regions?
  - **Data:**
    - ALMA CO(3-2) and rest-frame `803 micron` maps of SMM J0658 at `z=2.7768`, with a JWST-based lens model.
  - **Method:**
    - Reconstruct source-plane maps and compare CO-to-dust ratios in apertures centered on each tracer.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The inferred decorrelation scale is `0.8 +/- 0.1 kpc`; clump depletion times span `0.82-2.56 Gyr` under adopted conversions.
    - Inclination, blended regions and dust as an SFR proxy limit the cloud-evolution interpretation. One galaxy does not establish a universal new star-formation law.
  - **Position:** Provides a resolved gas-cycle counterpart to the large-scale environmental studies.
