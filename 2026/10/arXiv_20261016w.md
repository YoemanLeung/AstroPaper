## Key Findings

- **AGN environment signals depend on the activity tracer.** [2610.19320](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19320) Danyang Jiang finds denser surroundings for radio-bright AGN after host matching; [2610.19317](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19317) Shouyi Wang finds no X-ray companions in a z=6.63 quasar field. The different selections, redshifts, and limits prevent treating these as a single evolutionary sequence.
- **Gas-stack interpretation needs the galaxy population as well as gas physics.** [2610.17200](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17200) Lurdes Ondaro-Mallea demonstrates a satellite-induced kinetic-SZ bias in mocks; [2610.21628](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21628) Sanskriti Das measures richness-dependent thermal pressure. Together they motivate matched halo and membership models, but one result does not numerically correct the other.
- **Primitive and processed material coexist across early epochs.** [2610.16509](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16509) Sarah E. I. Bosman measures very weak enrichment at z=5.14; [2610.20722](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20722) Giulia Rodighiero detects structured dust attenuation at z=9.33. These distinct systems trace chemical diversity, not a universal chronological progression; [2610.12591](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12591) Omima Osman supplies a conditional grain-growth explanation for selected populations.
- **Strong emission and dense environments are useful clues, not unique physical diagnoses.** [2610.21232](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21232) Lena Pless finds that Lyalpha EW alone is a poor metallicity selector, while [2610.19937](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19937) Tianmu Gao cannot separate halo mass from stellar mass in assembly histories. Multivariate measurements and explicit selection remain necessary.
- **Cold-gas geometry is the next discriminating measurement.** [2610.20704](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20704) Damir Gasymov supports external supply without directly detecting the proposed filament stream. The imaging and multi-line requirements in [2610.21776](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21776) Kirsty M. Butler and survey forecasts in [2610.20885](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20885) Manuel Aravena outline how future observations could test gas-flow origins; those capabilities remain proposed.

## Survey foundations and environment-selected accretion

- [2610.13920](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13920) Shuqi Fu et al. `A Comprehensive JWST Slitless Spectroscopic Census of ~12,000 Emission-Line Galaxies at Redshift 0~9 in COSMOS`
  - **Question:** What makes a wide grism catalog useful for environment measurements?
  - **Data:**
    - COSMOS-3D NIRCam F444W slitless spectroscopy over 0.33 square degrees, with HST/JWST and ground-based photometry.
  - **Method:**
    - Combine direct line searches, template matching, image-spectrum morphology, and LLM-assisted identification followed by human review.
    - Assign redshift-quality ranks and contamination flags; check a subset with additional grism bands.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The catalog contains `12,275` sources over `z~0-9`. In the extra-band subset, `41/726` redshifts change and four sources are spurious; this is not an unbiased completeness measurement.
    - Environment analyses require footprint masks and exclusion of added sources with `ID>1,000,000`. Single-line ambiguities remain, and public release is planned upon publication.
  - **Position:** Provides a shared spectroscopic foundation for multi-tracer environment work, with selection and identification still central.

- [2610.19320](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19320) Danyang Jiang et al. `Large-scale Environmental Dependence of AGN Activity Revealed by JWST Paalpha Emitters`
  - **Question:** Which AGN populations occupy denser star-forming environments after host matching?
  - **Data:**
    - COSMOS-3D Pa-alpha emitters and 70 parent AGN at z~1.1-1.5; broad-line/X-ray selection plus radio measurements.
  - **Method:**
    - Measure completeness- and footprint-corrected eighth-neighbor densities in a +/-2000 km/s window.
    - Match stellar mass and star-formation rate; compare radio-bright, radio-faint, and full samples with controls.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Radio-bright AGN have median density excess `0.14 dex` (`KS p=0.003`); the full and radio-faint samples show no corresponding excess.
    - The mean scale is `1.5 physical Mpc`; two field-corner AGN are excluded at the density step. Pa-alpha counts trace star-forming neighbors, not gas supply itself, so the association does not prove environment-driven fueling.
  - **Position:** A controlled environmental comparison that illustrates why AGN subclasses cannot be pooled indiscriminately.

- [2610.19317](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19317) Shouyi Wang et al. `Deep X-ray View of a Distant Protocluster Anchored by a Quasar at $z=6.63$: No Evidence for Enhanced X-ray AGN Activity`
  - **Question:** Does a quasar-selected protocluster contain widespread X-ray-bright accretion?
  - **Data:**
    - Chandra: 865.5 ks cleaned exposure, 21 redshift-selected galaxies at 6.53<z<6.73, excluding the selection quasar.
  - **Method:**
    - Use position-dependent X-ray sensitivity and aperture photometry for individual detections and stacks.
    - Compute an incidence bound while distinguishing the sample mean from individual luminosity thresholds.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - No member is detected. The exposure-weighted mean rest-frame 2-10 keV luminosity is `<9.54e42 erg/s` at 90% confidence; the central 90% Jeffreys interval gives `f_AGN<0.09`.
    - These limits apply to detectable X-ray activity in a broad redshift window. Obscuration, intrinsic X-ray weakness, intermittent activity, and moderate-luminosity subsets remain allowed; the result is not absence of black-hole growth.
  - **Position:** A deep reionization-era environment constraint whose meaning depends on the accretion-selection channel.


## Early chemical diversity and galaxy analogues

- [2610.16509](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16509) Sarah E. I. Bosman et al. `Nearly pristine gas in a galaxy 1 billion years after the Big Bang`
  - **Question:** Can nearly pristine neutral gas survive after reionization?
  - **Data:**
    - Gemini/GHOST spectra of J1941-4500: 5.8 hr at resolving power 57,000, probing a damped Lyalpha absorber at z=5.14.
  - **Method:**
    - Fit unsaturated C, O, and Si lines with thermal/turbulent broadening.
    - Fit neutral hydrogen together with the neighboring absorber and multiple quasar-continuum realizations.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - At `z=5.139987`, the neutral column is `log(N_HI/cm^-2)=20.542 (+0.071/-0.014)` and carbon abundance is `[C/H]=-4.05 (+0.03/-0.08)` on the adopted solar scale.
    - The low fine-structure cooling discriminant favors top-heavy star formation under that criterion, but dust cooling can permit low-mass stars. C/O/Si do not distinguish Population III from extremely metal-poor Population II enrichment.
  - **Position:** Directly constrains a surviving low-enrichment gas reservoir, without identifying its stellar progenitors.

- [2610.21232](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21232) Lena Pless et al. `Are High-EW LAEs good analogs for the earliest galaxies? A study on the ionizing conditions and gas properties of LAEs at 2<=z<=7`
  - **Question:** Does strong Lyman-alpha emission reliably identify the most metal-poor galaxies?
  - **Data:**
    - MUSE-selected, HST-faint Lyman-alpha emitters (LAEs): 88 targets at 2<=z<=7, with NIRSpec spectroscopy and NIRCam imaging; 16 retain both reliable masses and metallicities.
  - **Method:**
    - Remeasure rest-frame equivalent widths (EWs) and fit stellar populations jointly with photometry and spectra.
    - Fit optical emission-line fluxes with BPASS+Cloudy grids; test parameter recovery and compare metallicity at fixed stellar mass.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - EW alone has large metallicity scatter: the linear test gives `r=-0.12, p=0.51`, while the rank test suggests a moderate trend (`rho=-0.56, p=0.015`). This is not evidence for a robust one-parameter metallicity selector.
    - High-EW systems tend to be under-enriched at fixed mass. Optical-only fits leave density and ionization degenerate; slit offsets and faintness strongly reduce the usable sample. Intermediate sample counts differ between Table 1 and the text; use the consistent 16-object joint sample for mass-metallicity conclusions.
  - **Position:** Tests the proposed reionization-analogue selection directly; stellar mass adds information beyond Lyalpha EW.

- [2610.20722](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20722) Giulia Rodighiero et al. `An Attenuation Bump at z=9.3: Probing Dust Diversity at Cosmic Dawn`
  - **Question:** What does a UV attenuation feature reveal about dust at cosmic dawn?
  - **Data:**
    - ZENITH/EMBER NIRSpec spectroscopy plus HST/NIRCam photometry of the Black Pearl at z=9.3335; PRISM integration 1.5 hr.
  - **Method:**
    - Fit a local power-law continuum plus Drude attenuation profile; test line contamination and wavelength windows.
    - Fit the full spectral energy distribution with a flexible attenuation law and two star-formation histories.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The local fit gives a bump at `2132+/-17 Angstrom`, width `413+/-54 Angstrom`, and peak attenuation `0.21+/-0.02 mag`.
    - The offset from 2175 Angstrom is only `~2.5 sigma` in that fit. Full-spectrum fits recover the bump with broader wavelength uncertainties that include the Galactic value. Small graphite grains and aromatic molecules remain viable; some attenuation and ionization parameters are weakly constrained.
  - **Position:** Extends direct UV-bump spectroscopy to z>9 without uniquely identifying the grain carrier or dust-production channel.

- [2610.12591](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12591) Omima Osman et al. `When dust growth takes over: the origin of dust in post-reionization galaxies`
  - **Question:** Which dust-formation process can reproduce post-reionization dust reservoirs?
  - **Data:**
    - 18 massive, UV-bright ALPINE-CRISTAL-JWST galaxies at z~4.5-5.7 and matched DUSTY-GAEA model populations.
  - **Method:**
    - Select model analogues by stellar mass, star-formation rate, and metallicity; compare dust-to-stellar, dust-to-gas, and dust-to-metal ratios.
    - Track progenitors and switch growth/destruction channels to isolate their contributions.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Efficient grain growth is required under the adopted supernova-destruction prescription; switching off both growth and destruction can also reach the observed dust masses.
    - The model transition near `z~8-9` applies to selected progenitors. Approximately `60%` of dust constraints are upper limits, and dust-temperature assumptions plus sample selection limit extrapolation to all early galaxies.
  - **Position:** A conditional enrichment model that complements, rather than explains uniquely, the observed z>9 attenuation feature.


## Gas thermodynamics, disk growth, and quenching

- [2610.17200](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17200) Lurdes Ondaro-Mallea et al. `Interpreting the stacked kinetic SZ signal II: the degeneracy between satellite galaxies and baryonic feedback`
  - **Question:** Can satellites mimic feedback in stacked kinetic Sunyaev-Zeldovich signals?
  - **Data:**
    - FLAMINGO feedback variants with DESI-like bright, luminous-red, and emission-line samples; ACT-like beam and observed covariance.
  - **Method:**
    - Separate central and satellite contributions to stacked gas optical-depth profiles.
    - Fit mixed-population mocks with a central-only emulator at fixed sample mean halo mass.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Satellites contribute roughly `50-80%` of the stacked signal in these mocks. Omitting them underestimates gas fractions by about a factor of `2` for the bright sample and `40%` for luminous red galaxies.
    - The emission-line sample has no significant bias at current errors. Bias size depends on selection, halo definition, beam, and fixed-mass assumptions; this is not a direct reanalysis of observed DESI/ACT gas fractions.
  - **Position:** Identifies a population-mixing degeneracy that must be controlled before interpreting gas profiles as feedback strength.

- [2610.21628](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21628) Sanskriti Das et al. `Thermal Sunyaev-Zel'dovich Effect in the circumgalactic medium - III: does richness matter?`
  - **Question:** Does halo thermal pressure depend on the number of member galaxies as well as halo mass?
  - **Data:**
    - ACT DR4 plus Planck thermal Sunyaev-Zeldovich maps and about 7 million DESI Legacy Imaging Survey halos at z<=1, divided by mass and richness.
  - **Method:**
    - Stack with halo-mass/redshift uncertainty weights and bootstrap covariance.
    - Fit one-halo, two-halo, and background terms; compare pressure slopes and offsets before integrating thermal energy.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - For isolated galaxies the fitted thermal-energy relation is `7.3 sigma` shallower than self-similarity; group slopes remain consistent with self-similarity.
    - Some bins require minimum temperatures of `~2-7` times the virial temperature if the cosmic baryon fraction bounds the gas mass. These are conditional floors, not direct temperatures. Halo masses, unresolved profiles, richness selection, and dust correction limit feedback interpretation; thermal SZ alone does not determine gas density.
  - **Position:** Makes richness an explicit variable in circumgalactic thermodynamics and motivates matched samples across gas probes.

- [2610.20704](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20704) Damir Gasymov et al. `CoRGI in MaNGA - II. NGC 5717: cold-gas accretion and disk growth in a BGG`
  - **Question:** Can an old group-central galaxy grow a young disk from externally supplied gas?
  - **Data:**
    - MaNGA spectroscopy, CFHT imaging, H I constraints, and SDSS/DESI group spectroscopy of NGC 5717.
  - **Method:**
    - Separate two stellar components in full-spectrum fits, excluding the degenerate central region.
    - Compare stellar populations and gas kinematics with a chemical-evolution model and the surrounding filament geometry.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The counter-rotating disk has age `1.35+/-0.16 Gyr`, `[Fe/H]=-0.67+/-0.09`, and `log(M_star/M_sun)=9.9+/-0.1`, favoring in-situ formation from external gas.
    - Filament accretion is a preferred interpretation, not a directly detected stream. The overparameterized chemical model admits multiple infall histories, and unusual gas-rich donors cannot be excluded.
  - **Position:** Shows that substantial disk regrowth can occur in a group-central system, while separating the acquired gas from its inferred origin.

- [2610.19937](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19937) Tianmu Gao et al. `The Role of Environment in Shaping the Assembly Timelines of Massive Quiescent Galaxies at Cosmic Noon`
  - **Question:** Does environment independently change the assembly times of quiescent galaxies at cosmic noon?
  - **Data:**
    - Blue Jay spectroscopy and imaging of 12 massive quiescent galaxies; 11 have usable 3D-HST halo-mass estimates.
  - **Method:**
    - Infer nonparametric star-formation histories with continuity and bursty priors.
    - Compare formation/quenching epochs with halo masses and test partial correlations controlling stellar mass.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Continuity-prior median formation redshifts are `9.86` and `3.90` in the high- and low-halo-mass groups, but the formation-halo rank test has `p=0.10`.
    - Halo mass and stellar mass correlate strongly (`r=0.95`); controlled halo trends are not significant. Small samples, history priors, and model-based halo estimates prevent a robust independent environment claim.
  - **Position:** A suggestive assembly-environment link whose present evidence does not separate halo and stellar mass.


## Black-hole evolution and future cold-gas tests

- [2610.20709](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20709) Zihao Wang et al. `How Little Red Dots Turn Blue: Evolution of Kelvin-Helmholtz Contracting Black Hole Envelopes and Observational Diagnostics`
  - **Question:** Can contracting black-hole envelopes connect little red dots to their blue counterparts?
  - **Data:**
    - Semi-analytic envelope models: 2,500 feedback/retention combinations compared with published broad-line spectra.
  - **Method:**
    - Evolve envelope mass and energy under declining gas supply, black-hole accretion, and feedback.
    - Post-process photospheric and stellar light; track UV and optical slopes.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The models predict a red UV excursion before the blue phase; transitional durations span roughly `5` to `>50 Myr`, depending on feedback and gas retention.
    - This is a testable evolutionary hypothesis, not a measured population sequence. Strong feedback can violate the quasi-hydrostatic photosphere assumption; fixed stellar histories and a single accretion episode limit abundance predictions.
  - **Position:** Provides color-space and lifetime predictions for black-hole-envelope interpretations of little red dots.

- [2610.21776](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21776) Kirsty M. Butler et al. `ALMA2040: The High-Redshift Circumgalactic Medium. Imaging the fueling and feedback of galaxies in the early universe`
  - **Question:** What observations could separate cold circumgalactic inflow, outflow, and recycled gas at high redshift?
  - **Data:**
    - ALMA2040 white paper using published lensed galaxies, extended [C II] halos, and CRISTAL stacks as benchmarks; no new population census.
  - **Method:**
    - Translate current surface-brightness and spatial-resolution limits into requirements for individual z~4-8 halos and clump-scale winds.
    - Combine emission-line cubes with absorption and multiple species to distinguish gas phases and flow directions.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The case requires roughly `5-10x` better line sensitivity, diffuse-gas recovery beyond `10 kpc`, and compact-structure resolution near `100 pc`.
    - Longer baselines alone are insufficient: short spacings and total-power data are essential. Emission kinematics alone leave radial-flow direction ambiguous; baseline and surface-brightness trade studies remain necessary.
  - **Position:** A facility requirements case linking cold-gas imaging to the unresolved physical origin of halo emission.

- [2610.20885](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20885) Manuel Aravena et al. `Unveiling the Baryon Cycle Through Cosmic Time`
  - **Question:** How could wide-band molecular surveys measure the baryon cycle across galaxy populations?
  - **Data:**
    - ALMA2040 forecasts anchored to ASPECS line luminosity functions and an Arp 220-based spectral template.
  - **Method:**
    - Simulate multi-line spectra and forecast three survey tiers with assumed future sensitivity and spectral coverage.
    - Propagate line-excitation and luminosity-function assumptions to detection thresholds and sample yields.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - A proposed `~0.24 deg^2` mosaic reaches ASPECS-like line depth in `~90 hr` on source (`~180 hr` with assumed overheads); an ultra-deep tier covers `4-5 arcmin^2`.
    - These are forecasts. Yield uncertainties are estimated at `~30%` and larger for the ultra-deep tier, which extrapolates luminosity functions about one decade below current constraints; single-template spectra do not represent all galaxy populations.
  - **Position:** Connects a proposed sensitivity/bandwidth gain to explicit survey area, depth, and cosmic-variance trade-offs.
