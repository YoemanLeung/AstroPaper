## Key Findings

- Radio AGN are more prevalent in groups at fixed stellar mass, but abundance enhancements and per-galaxy fractions are different statistics; volume assumptions affect the former. [2610.10719](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10719) F. Ubertosi.
- Selection uncertainty remains central to early-galaxy abundances: the CAPERS prior test improves redshift success within a targeted sample, while the UVLF synthesis exposes correlated measurements and unresolved high-redshift systematics. [2610.12322](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12322) Callum T. Donnan; [2610.12121](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12121) A. Fontana.
- Black-hole seed prescriptions can strongly affect early assembly without fixing the final central mass. Convergence in one exceptionally dense simulated region is not a population-wide result. [2610.12398](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12398) Yihao Zhou.
- Cold-gas interpretation requires both thermal history and tracer calibration: molecular gas survives in a weak cool core, while model CO luminosities respond sharply to metallicity-dependent shielding. [2610.10721](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10721) Prathamesh Tamhane; [2610.12159](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12159) Doga Tolgay.
- An observed environment or flow statistic need not isolate the desired physics: small-cell counts mix occupation noise with density, and ring averages can conceal strong local gas transport. [2610.10936](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10936) Raymon Isaac; [2610.11588](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11588) Yi He.

## Environment and gas supply

- [2610.10719](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10719) F. Ubertosi et al. `Prevalence of radio Active Galactic Nuclei in galaxy groups since z=3.5 in the COSMOS-Web field`
  - **Question:** Does group environment enhance radio AGN activity at fixed stellar mass?
  - **Data:**
    - VLA-COSMOS 3 GHz radio-excess AGN matched to COSMOS-Web galaxies and groups at `0.1<=z<=3.5` and `10<=log(Mstar/Msun)<=11.4`.
  - **Method:**
    - Assign group membership at association probability `>=0.5`; estimate environment-specific luminosity functions and radio-completeness-corrected AGN fractions.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Group radio AGN fractions average about twice the field values at fixed stellar mass; significance varies across redshift bins. Fractions peak within `0.2 R200`.
    - The `3-30` space-density enhancement depends on the adopted group volume. Cosmic variance in one `~0.45 deg^2` field and photometric membership limit generalization; a fueling transition near `z~1.5` is an interpretation.
  - **Position:** Extends radio-environment tests beyond the lower-redshift group and cluster samples discussed in the manuscript.

- [2610.10721](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10721) Prathamesh Tamhane et al. `NGC 4936: abundant multi-phase medium in the central weak X-ray cool core and optically dark H I clouds in the group`
  - **Question:** Can a weak cool core retain molecular gas above a simple precipitation threshold?
  - **Data:**
    - ALMA CO(1-0), MeerKAT HI, Chandra X-rays, MUSE/SOAR ionised gas and optical imaging of NGC 4936 and its group.
  - **Method:**
    - Fit the molecular disk and thermodynamic profiles; compare central HI limits, group clouds and interaction morphology.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The central molecular reservoir is `(1.93+/-0.05)x10^8 Msun` under a Galactic conversion factor; central HI is below `1.13x10^7 Msun` at `3 sigma`.
    - Twenty HI structures lie in the wider environment. Cold gas coexists with present-day `tcool/tff~25`; atmospheric cooling is plausible, but past conditions, interactions and external acquisition are not uniquely separable.
  - **Position:** Tests multiphase condensation in a group where a single present-day cooling threshold is an incomplete account of gas history.

- [2610.10936](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10936) Raymon Isaac et al. `The conditional colour-magnitude distribution: III. A study of galaxy colour- and luminosity-dependent counts-in-cells statistics`
  - **Question:** When do galaxy counts in cells measure environment rather than halo-occupation noise?
  - **Data:**
    - SDSS DR7 galaxies in `16` color-luminosity bins and CCMD mocks calibrated to two-point clustering.
  - **Method:**
    - Match number densities, account for survey boundaries, and compare void probabilities and counts with CCMD and analytic distributions.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Small or sparsely populated cells mix matter-density fluctuations with stochastic halo occupation; large or well-populated cells are more directly environment dominated.
    - Some luminous subsamples disagree at `R=5 h^-1 Mpc`. Removing the Sloan Great Wall does not generally resolve the mismatch; velocity-bias adjustments introduce their own calibration dependence.
  - **Position:** Shows why a small-scale galaxy-density statistic cannot by itself establish galaxy assembly bias.

## Early galaxies and black-hole seeds

- [2610.12322](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12322) Callum T. Donnan et al. `JWST CAPERS: Spectroscopic evaluation of very high redshift galaxy candidates`
  - **Question:** Which photometric choices most reliably select very high-redshift galaxies?
  - **Data:**
    - JWST CAPERS NIRSpec/PRISM and NIRCam photometry: `327` targeted candidates, `183` secure spectroscopic redshifts, and `48` confirmed at `z>=8.5`.
  - **Method:**
    - Measure emission-line or Lyman-break redshifts; compare template, SNR, break-strength and chi-squared cuts with a UV-luminosity-function prior.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The LF prior raises the defined photometric-redshift success fraction from `~35%` to `~77%`, while sample-relative completeness falls from `~79%` to `~69%`.
    - The highest redshift measured by this analysis is `12.98`. The `144` targets without secure redshifts and deliberately heterogeneous candidate selection prevent interpreting these fractions as population-wide purity or completeness; LF inference still needs selection simulations.
  - **Position:** Turns photometric selection choices into spectroscopically testable tradeoffs; the prior changes redshift probabilities rather than merely imposing another quality cut.

- [2610.12121](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12121) A. Fontana et al. `Too many or too bright? Status and perspectives in the study of the UV Luminosity Function at z>10`
  - **Question:** What evolution of the UV luminosity function is supported beyond redshift nine?
  - **Data:**
    - Published JWST UVLF measurements over `8.5<z<14.5`, spectroscopic lower limits, and competing candidate/upper-limit results at `z~17`.
  - **Method:**
    - Fit a smoothly evolving Schechter function and separate redshift bins; compare UV luminosity densities and alternative galaxy-formation prescriptions.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - A four-parameter descriptive fit gives `alpha~-2.3`, `Mstar_UV~-21.7` and a normalization decline of `~0.17 dex` per unit redshift.
    - Shared imaging makes many literature points non-independent; inter-study scatter of `~0.1-0.2 dex` and parameter degeneracy limit physical interpretation. The evidence above `z~15` does not yet decide between continued smooth evolution and a sharp decline.
  - **Position:** A useful synthesis of JWST abundance constraints and model degeneracies, rather than a new independent census.

- [2610.12398](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12398) Yihao Zhou et al. `ACORN I. Massive Black Hole Seeding and Tidal Disruption Events from Star Clusters in Cosmological Simulations`
  - **Question:** How does star-cluster-based seeding change early black-hole assembly?
  - **Data:**
    - Four ASTRID-based runs of one constrained `5-sigma` overdensity, evolved to `z=6`, comparing cluster, gas and halo seeding.
  - **Method:**
    - Populate unresolved star clusters, assign collision-motivated seed masses, evolve clusters and tidal disruptions, and compare runs with/without recoil.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Allowing multiple seeds per halo produces a clustered population and faster early assembly. In the dominant halo, different prescriptions converge near `10^8.6 Msun` by `z=6` as gas accretion regulates growth.
    - This is a controlled rare-environment experiment, not a cosmological merger-rate forecast. Cluster dynamics are subgrid, numerical mergers occur on kiloparsec scales, and recoil assumes zero spin.
  - **Position:** Connects seed formation to local cluster properties instead of prescribing one fixed-mass seed per eligible halo.

## Gas diagnostics and the baryon cycle

- [2610.12159](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12159) Doga Tolgay et al. `Modeling CO Line Luminosity Using FIRE Simulations: I. Methodology and Validation of Molecular Gas and CO Abundance`
  - **Question:** How reliably can CO and molecular gas be predicted from simulated galaxies?
  - **Data:**
    - FIRE-2 galaxy suites, Milky Way clouds and sight lines, xCOLD GASS, PHIBSS2 and ASPECS comparisons.
  - **Method:**
    - Use SKIRT radiation fields and a five-parameter CLOUDY grid; interpolate line emission and species abundances per gas cell.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Changing assumed cloud metallicity from solar to three times solar changes summed predicted CO luminosity by about an order of magnitude.
    - At `z=0`, molecular masses overlap xCOLD GASS scatter for `SFR>=0.3 Msun/yr`; lower-SFR coverage is insufficient for a decisive test. High-redshift comparisons remain limited by CO conversion and survey selection.
  - **Position:** Provides a physical forward-model component for CO surveys and intensity mapping; galaxy-wide CO-luminosity validation is deferred to the companion paper.

- [2610.11588](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11588) Yi He et al. `Measuring radial flows in disc galaxies`
  - **Question:** Can radial gas flows be recovered without absorbing their signal into disk warps?
  - **Data:**
    - Idealized HI cubes and mock observations of `12` selected Milky Way analogues from TNG50 at `z=0`.
  - **Method:**
    - Use a four-step 3D Barolo workflow, jointly fit radial velocity and warp geometry, and estimate uncertainties by bootstrap.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Joint fitting recovers ring-averaged radial motions more reliably than fixing the warp first; performance deteriorates at only four resolution elements per side.
    - The selected TNG50 disks have substantial local inflows and outflows but no coherent average radial trend. Axisymmetric recovery cannot measure all transport along bars, arms or tidal features.
  - **Position:** A validated method for synthetic data, with a clear distinction between azimuthal averages and local gas transport.

- [2610.12394](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12394) Kai Wang et al. `Inflow-driven galaxy evolution - II: A hierarchy in the scaling relations of star-forming galaxies`
  - **Question:** Can one gas-flow framework explain the hierarchy of galaxy scaling relations?
  - **Data:**
    - An ideal analytic gas-flow model and a cosmological extension, including `20000` tracks with independent `0.15 dex` parameter offsets.
  - **Method:**
    - Solve gas and metal continuity equations; vary halo formation time, star formation efficiency and mass loading to track scatter and residual correlations.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Within this model, halo formation time primarily sets sSFR; star formation efficiency adds gas-fraction scatter, and mass loading adds metallicity scatter.
    - The result uses metal-free inflow, instantaneous recycling, no enriched re-accretion and no mergers. It is an explanatory hierarchy, not a unique empirical decomposition of the baryon cycle.
  - **Position:** Connects the main sequence, gas fractions and metallicity through explicit continuity equations and interpretable assumptions.

## Resolved dual AGN

- [2610.10687](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10687) M. Scialpi et al. `Cosmic Duets II. Growth and accretion in Gaia Multi-Peak dual AGN at Cosmic Noon`
  - **Question:** Do close dual AGN share accretion power as predicted by merger models?
  - **Data:**
    - Thirty Gaia Multi-Peak dual AGN at `0.5<=z<=3` and projected separations `1-7 kpc`, with spatially resolved spectroscopy.
  - **Method:**
    - Decompose component spectra; estimate bolometric luminosities and single-epoch virial masses; impose GMP-like selection on comparison models.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Observed pairs have more balanced black-hole masses and accretion power than L-GalaxiesBH predictions, suggesting stronger secondary growth or a longer secondary-active phase.
    - Two systems cannot enter the virial-mass analysis. Broad-line mass systematics, optical selection and the different Astrid redshift distribution constrain the interpretation.
  - **Position:** Tests merger-fueling prescriptions with resolved pairs rather than treating dual activity as an automatic measure of merger enhancement.
