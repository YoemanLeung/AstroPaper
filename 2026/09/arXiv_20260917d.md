## Key Findings

- Dust-obscured galaxies can enrich the early CGM while escaping UV selection; the absorber-host association is firmer than the inferred wind rate or Pop III origin. The `~50 km/s` calibration uncertainty is comparable to the absorption centroid shift ([2609.17680](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17680) Qinyue Fei).
- Galaxy-pair dynamics favors ordinary halo masses with somewhat elevated stellar conversion efficiency at cosmic dawn, but the current offset is not significant evidence against TNG100 ([2609.17756](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17756) Zihao Wu).
- Different tracers expose different gas phases: X-ray brightness may miss pressure-bearing halo gas, while narrow HI at shell edges cannot alone distinguish newly cooled from swept-up material. The SZ excess probabilities are only `86-94%` ([2609.18006](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18006) Jiang-Tao Li; [2609.18160](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18160) Shin-Jeong Kim).
- Structural correlations survive some controls but do not establish causation: compactness tracks nebular density at matched mass/SFR, and morphology-related cosmic-web segregation weakens toward dwarf masses. The latter result concerns groups and the field, and should not be generalized to rich clusters ([2609.18000](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18000) Shihong Liu; [2609.17662](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17662) Ilin Lazar).
- Spectroscopic association can overturn a feedback narrative: a supposed fossil radio lobe belongs to a background galaxy, removing a key argument for a long interruption in the foreground AGN ([2609.18543](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18543) M. Orienti).

## Early metal enrichment and galaxy-halo growth

- [2609.17680](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17680) Qinyue Fei: A dense, metal-rich absorber driven by a heavily dust-obscured galaxy: The direct evidence of CGM enrichment at z~7
  - **Question:** Can a dusty galaxy be associated directly with enriched circumgalactic gas during reionization?
  - **Data:**
    - JWST/NIRSpec and NOEMA: absorption toward GNz7q and nebular/[CII] emission from ND1 at `z_sys=7.0321 +/- 0.0007`, with projected separation `16 kpc`.
  - **Method:**
    - Combine systemic-redshift measurements, absorption-line curve-of-growth analysis, and host SED fitting.
    - Estimate wind transport using assumed metallicity, covering fraction, and geometry.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The association probes gas inside the estimated halo virial radius. Absorption centroids are mildly blueshifted; wavelength-calibration systematics of `~50 km/s` matter.
    - The fiducial rate is `~64 Msun/yr` (plausible range `23-260 Msun/yr`), using `v_out~300 km/s`, `[Si/H]=-1.5`, and covering fraction `0.5`. Loading `~3` uses the `100 Myr`-averaged SFR (`26 +/- 10 Msun/yr`), not the instantaneous `168 +/- 35 Msun/yr`.
    - Saturation limits the abundance inference. Enhanced C/O is compatible with primordial yields but does not uniquely identify Pop III enrichment.
  - **Position:** Extends high-redshift absorber-host work to a UV-faint dusty system; the XQR-30 comparison supplies ionization context.

- [2609.17756](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17756) Zihao Wu: JADES: Dynamical Measurements of Dark Matter Halo Masses at z ~ 7 Using Galaxy Pairs
  - **Question:** Can first-infall galaxy pairs constrain halo masses during cosmic dawn?
  - **Data:**
    - JADES: `10` galaxy-pair systems at `z=6-8` with [OIII] spectroscopy.
    - TNG100: calibration and mock-observation tests of dynamical estimators.
  - **Method:**
    - Model pair velocities and separations; marginalize over projection, measurement errors, and intrinsic scatter.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - At `log(Mstar/Msun)=9`, the inferred mean is `log(M200/Msun)=11.06 +/- 0.21`, corresponding to integrated efficiency `5(+3/-2)%`.
    - The stellar-mass offset from TNG100 is `~0.3 dex` at fixed halo mass, only `1.3 sigma`. Calibration is most accurate near the virial radius; estimator scatter is `~0.2 dex`.
  - **Position:** Provides a dynamical complement to abundance matching and clustering, subject to pair selection and simulation calibration.

- [2609.17667](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17667) A. Ferrara: On the clumpy nature of super-early galaxies
  - **Question:** Can disk instability explain the masses, sizes, and visibility of early stellar clumps?
  - **Data:**
    - Published JWST clump measurements and an analytic model of unstable, gas-rich high-redshift disks.
  - **Method:**
    - Integrate the unstable Toomre modes to obtain a clump spectrum.
    - Apply gas dynamical friction and compare supernova and trapped-radiation clearing.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The model spans `log(Mcl/Msun)~5.5-7.3`; observed mass-size relations are consistent with turbulence `~10-80 km/s`.
    - Preferential loss of massive clumps reproduces a mass-function slope near `-1.89`. Radiation pressure clears dense natal gas in the model; supernovae alone do not.
    - Later stellar expansion, stripping, and dissolution are not followed, so present-day cluster survival is not predicted.
  - **Position:** Extends classical Toomre fragmentation beyond a single fastest-growing scale to a population and visibility model.


## Gas phases, feedback, and host identification

- [2609.18006](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18006) Jiang-Tao Li: A Possible Hidden Hot Halo Component Revealed by Joint X-ray and Sunyaev--Zel'dovich Observations
  - **Question:** Does the X-ray-emitting phase account for the pressure of a massive galaxy halo?
  - **Data:**
    - XMM-Newton XART-ATOMS observations of NGC 4594 and multiple Planck-based SZ reconstructions.
  - **Method:**
    - Predict the SZ profile from X-ray density/temperature measurements and compare with reconstructed pressure.
    - Explore two phases in pressure equilibrium and alternative thermal/nonthermal explanations.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Near `50 kpc`, the probabilities that SZ exceeds the X-ray prediction are `~86%` and `~94%` for two map selections; equality is not strongly excluded.
    - At the most probable ratio, a two-phase model gives X-ray filling factor `~(4-6)e-3`, with possible mass and energy corrections of `~2.5` and `~10`. These are conditional estimates, not securely measured missing-baryon fractions.
  - **Position:** Joint density-weighted and pressure-weighted probes expose phase degeneracy in halo baryon inventories.

- [2609.18160](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18160) Shin-Jeong Kim: A Detailed GASKAP-HI View of Shells and Loops in the Magellanic Bridge
  - **Question:** Does a feedback-driven HI shell create cold gas or redistribute an existing cold phase?
  - **Data:**
    - GASKAP-HI: `~8 pc` resolution around a `~130 pc` shell in the Magellanic Bridge.
    - Herschel `250 micron`, Halpha, and CO information trace associated dust and gas.
  - **Method:**
    - Decompose HI profiles with spatially coherent Gaussian components.
    - Map lower limits on cold-gas columns using a Fourier method.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Narrow components with `sigma<2.5 km/s` trace shell walls, while the cavity is ionized. Cold clumps also occur along a larger loop.
    - A single snapshot cannot separate in situ cooling from swept-up cold gas; the larger structure supports a pre-existing contribution.
  - **Position:** Connects feedback to a tidally structured multiphase medium and applies the Marchal+2024 cold-HI mapping approach.

- [2609.17723](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17723) Erika B. Hoffman: Uncovering New Ionized Winds and Relativistic Hot Outflows in MCG-6-30-15 with Chandra HETG and a Bayesian Framework
  - **Question:** How does flexible ionization modeling change the inferred AGN wind census?
  - **Data:**
    - Chandra/HETG: `0.65 Ms` archival observations of MCG-6-30-15 from 2000 and 2004.
  - **Method:**
    - Fit broadband emission and Cloudy absorption grids in photoionization and collisional equilibrium.
    - Compare additional components with the Deviance Information Criterion.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The deeper observation supports `11` absorbing zones and an ultra-fast component at `~0.08c`. Several phases admit competing ionization explanations.
    - A near-systemic collisionally ionized component could be galactic/CGM gas. The maximum photoionized UFO power reaches `~5%` of bolometric luminosity, not a measured feedback coupling efficiency.
    - Missing oxygen atomic transitions can mimic cool relativistic components; those detections remain insecure.
  - **Position:** Reassesses legacy grating winds in the context of XRISM while exposing atomic-data limits on feedback inference.

- [2609.18543](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18543) M. Orienti: Challenging the archetypal intermittent radio galaxy J0111+3906. New insights from optical GTC/OSIRIS and radio VLA and LOFAR observations
  - **Question:** Is extended radio emission near J0111+3906 evidence for an earlier AGN episode?
  - **Data:**
    - GTC/OSIRIS+ spectroscopy and VLA/LOFAR imaging of J0111+3906 and the source `~20 arcsec` east.
  - **Method:**
    - Determine both optical redshifts and resolve the radio core/halo structure.
    - Compare radio spectra and equipartition radiative-age estimates.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The eastern galaxy lies at `z=1.042`, behind the CSO at `z=0.668`; its radio emission cannot be a fossil lobe of the foreground system.
    - An eastern-source radiative age below `~4 Myr` is conditional on the spectral/equipartition model. A foreground low-frequency excess still permits more compact fossil plasma.
  - **Position:** Demonstrates why radio morphology alone cannot establish physical association or an AGN duty cycle.


## Cosmic-web environment and internal structure

- [2609.17662](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17662) Ilin Lazar: The relationship between morphology, density, and location in the cosmic web from massive to dwarf galaxies
  - **Question:** How does morphology-dependent cosmic-web segregation change toward dwarf masses?
  - **Data:**
    - HST visual morphology for a mass-complete sample of `~13,000` galaxies at `0.2<z<0.4`, spanning `10^8-10^11.5 Msun`.
    - The sample covers groups and the field, rather than rich clusters.
  - **Method:**
    - Use DisPerSE to measure projected distances to nodes/filaments and compare morphology classes across stellar mass and local density.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The body and Figures 6-7 place massive early types closer to nodes/filaments than late types; their distances converge toward `Mstar~10^9 Msun`. The abstract reverses the high-mass distance ordering.
    - Lower-mass galaxies of either morphology lie farther from filament cores. Reduced distance segregation does not imply that all environmental effects disappear.
  - **Position:** Extends the mass/cosmic-web analyses of Laigle+2018 and Kraljic+2018 to a dwarf-complete morphology sample.

- [2609.18000](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18000) Shihong Liu: Does galaxy-scale compactness regulate nebular electron density? A cross-scale test with DESI
  - **Question:** Does nebular density retain a dependence on galaxy compactness at fixed mass and SFR?
  - **Data:**
    - DESI DR1 AGN-clean star-forming galaxies at `0.10<z<0.44`, with [SII] doublets and stellar sizes/masses.
  - **Method:**
    - Divide each redshift interval into compactness tertiles and match mass, then joint mass/SFR distributions.
    - Convert stacked [SII] ratios into density and bootstrap galaxies; vary the assumed electron temperature.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - After joint mass/SFR matching, high-minus-low compactness contrasts are `38.0`, `56.1`, and `46.1 cm^-3` across the three redshift bins at `Te=10^4 K`.
    - The ordering survives tested temperature choices. Stack-inferred density is a population diagnostic; pressure confinement is an interpretation, not a direct causal measurement.
  - **Position:** Connects kpc-scale structure to nebular conditions through controlled comparisons rather than an assumed geometric identity.

- [2609.18221](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18221) Snehasish Bhattacharjee: Void probability function in the Quijote simulations
  - **Question:** Can void probabilities distinguish cosmologies independently of tracer selection?
  - **Data:**
    - Quijote halo catalogs and HOD-generated galaxy samples across standard and extended cosmologies.
  - **Method:**
    - Measure empty-cell probabilities while varying redshift, mass, dilution, and redshift-space positions.
    - Compare geometric-hierarchical and negative-binomial descriptions.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Halo statistics resemble the geometric-hierarchical model at `z=0`; higher redshift, dilution, and redshift-space effects move them toward the negative-binomial form.
    - Cosmological sensitivity is entangled with tracer properties and cosmic variance. HOD morphology comparisons do not constitute hydrodynamical galaxy-formation predictions.
  - **Position:** Offers an efficient higher-order clustering diagnostic whose survey use requires calibrated selection and covariance.

- [2609.19132](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19132) Yu-Chen Wang: Massive Galaxy Halos Contain Less Inner Dark Matter Than Predicted
  - **Question:** Do observed inner mass profiles agree with simulations at fixed independently estimated halo mass?
  - **Data:**
    - MaNGA stellar dynamics, ALFALFA HI kinematics, and independently calibrated SDSS group halo masses.
    - IllustrisTNG and EAGLE supply simulation comparisons.
  - **Method:**
    - Combine statistical constraints at stellar, HI, and halo scales; subtract baryonic contributions.
    - Test morphology, star formation, inclination, and stellar-mass assumptions.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Massive halos show lower inner dark-matter content than simulations, while lower-mass halos agree more closely. NFW-like profiles with lower effective concentrations remain allowed.
    - The quoted central-fraction difference of `~4 sigma` is in units of population scatter. Simulations have not been passed through matched survey mock observations, limiting significance and causal interpretation.
  - **Position:** Uses an external halo-mass scale to test baryonic redistribution; HI linewidths alone do not fix total halo mass.


## Survey products and inference tools

- [2609.17673](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17673) Shouyi Wang: Chandra Lensing-cluster Ultradeep Extragalactic Survey (CLUES) I: A 2 Ms Point-Source Catalog of the Abell 2744 Field
  - **Question:** What X-ray sensitivity and reliability are available in the Abell 2744 lensing field?
  - **Data:**
    - CLUES: `101` Chandra/ACIS-I observations, `2.094 Ms` cleaned exposure, and `369 arcmin^2` area.
  - **Method:**
    - Detect sources in three bands, fit local PSFs, and screen structured intracluster emission.
    - Run end-to-end simulations to derive reliability, completeness, and sensitivity maps.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The catalog contains `327` sources; the soft-band `50%` completeness flux is `2.8e-16 erg/cm^2/s`.
    - Reliability is lower in the bright cluster core. Intrinsic source sensitivity depends on lensing and position; source classification and uniform multiwavelength counterparts are deferred to later work.
  - **Position:** Provides a calibrated X-ray counterpart resource for HST/JWST lensing-field studies alongside the Chandra Deep Fields.

- [2609.17636](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17636) A. Marchal: IViS: Interferometric Visibility-domain inversion Software - A GPU-accelerated Python framework for joint deconvolution with ASKAP
  - **Question:** Can joint visibility inversion preserve extended HI better than separately cleaned mosaics?
  - **Data:**
    - Point-source/noise/diffuse-sky simulations and ASKAP/GASKAP-HI observations toward the LMC; single-dish short spacings where available.
  - **Method:**
    - Fit one nonparametric sky cube with a GPU NUFFT forward model, spatial regularization, positivity, and optional single-dish fusion.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - LMC mosaics reach effective resolution `~22 arcsec`, with less residual sidelobe structure and more large-scale power than the compared ASKAPSoft product.
    - Regularization alters the effective beam. Gains over separate mosaicking depend on morphology; single-dish fusion is needed to constrain missing large scales.
  - **Position:** Extends joint-deconvolution ideas associated with Cornwell+1988 into a GPU-accelerated visibility-domain reconstruction workflow.

- [2609.18694](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18694) Mattia Vaccari: The Spitzer Data Fusion - A Far-Ultraviolet to Far-Infrared Multi-Wavelength Database in Spitzer Extragalactic Survey Fields
  - **Question:** Can legacy extragalactic photometry be merged into a reusable multiwavelength reference?
  - **Data:**
    - Spitzer Data Fusion: `4.4 million` IRAC-selected sources over `65 deg^2` in eight fields.
    - SERVS companion: `2.8 million` sources over `18 deg^2`.
  - **Method:**
    - Register catalogs to 2MASS, merge with wavelength-dependent matching radii, and attach redshift information and provenance.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Versioned FITS catalogs combine far-UV through far-IR photometry. DR1 is a legacy release documented by this manuscript, not a new observing campaign.
    - IRAC selection and resolution-dependent matches remain essential when constructing complete or deblended samples.
  - **Position:** A practical photometric bridge from Spitzer legacy fields to Euclid, Rubin, and SKA-precursor analyses.

- [2609.17658](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17658) Klaus Dolag: The OpenGadget3 Code for Cosmological Simulations
  - **Question:** Can divergent Gadget developments be consolidated into reproducible cosmological simulations?
  - **Data:**
    - OpenGadget3 public code, documented physical modules, numerical tests, and continuous-integration infrastructure.
  - **Method:**
    - Combine TreePM gravity with SPH or meshless-finite-mass hydrodynamics and configurable cooling, enrichment, star formation, and AGN modules.
    - Record parameters and changes and maintain a shared release/developer workflow.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - A broad module set is released with documentation and tests, reducing dependence on incompatible private branches.
    - Technical compatibility is not sufficient for physical consistency: combining explicit coolants with precomputed cooling tables can double-count processes.
  - **Position:** Consolidates the Gadget-2/3 lineage as infrastructure for controlled galaxy/CGM comparisons; no new observational constraint is implied.
