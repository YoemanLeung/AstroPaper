## Key Findings

- Most ICL stars formed outside the main halo but were stripped inside it ([2609.20560](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20560) S. V. Werner).
- The void size deficit strengthens in deeper underdensities; the Simba topology signal is clearest for satellites ([2609.19253](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19253) Olivia Curtis; [2609.19993](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19993) Ryusei R. Kano).
- Interacting dwarfs show burst-enhanced growth, while modeled outflow shells can fail to stop narrow inflowing streams ([2609.19687](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19687) Yoshihisa Asada; [2609.19265](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19265) Judah Luberto).
- Agreement in global averages can conceal local errors: diverse dust profiles leave ensemble SFRs nearly unchanged, while Balmer lines can miss fully obscured regions ([2609.19298](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19298) Ivana Barisic).
- Quasar diagnostics retain major degeneracies: Fe II templates affect inferred accretion, and a long-lived obscurer challenges simple thermal-wind models ([2609.19459](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19459) Anniek J. Gloudemans; [2609.19910](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19910) A. Akylas).

## Protocluster outskirts and early assembly

- [2609.20560](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20560) S. V. Werner: Intracluster light in the infall region of simulated protoclusters of galaxies
  - **Question:** Where did the stars making up protocluster intracluster light form and become unbound?
  - **Data:**
    - TNG-Cluster and Manhattan Suite: diffuse stars selected near `z=2` and traced through progenitor galaxies and environments.
  - **Method:**
    - Track stellar birth sites, stripping sites, host masses, and diffuse stellar mass in the main halo and infall region.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Infall regions contain `10^10-3x10^11 Msun` of diffuse stars at `2<z<4`. About `56-58%` of the stars in the `z=2` ICL formed in infall regions, but `69-74%` were stripped inside the main halo.
    - Host galaxies at stripping are `3-4` times more massive than at stellar birth. Stellar formation in the outskirts does not mean formation as unbound stars; detectability requires surface-brightness modeling.
  - **Position:** Connects diffuse-light assembly to protocluster outskirts; both simulation suites support separating stellar birth from later stripping.

- [2609.19687](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19687) Yoshihisa Asada: Early steps in the hierarchical assembly of a Milky Way-mass galaxy 1 Gyr after the Big Bang
  - **Question:** How much can interaction-driven star formation accelerate the assembly of an early dwarf group?
  - **Data:**
    - JWST/NIRCam and NIRSpec, plus HST: three strongly lensed galaxies behind MACS J0417.5-1154 at `z_spec=5.196`, with component masses of roughly `10^6-10^7.5 Msun`.
  - **Method:**
    - Combine lensing-corrected photometry, spectroscopy, burst histories, and abundance matching.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Projected separations of `~2 kpc` and velocity offsets from `-120 +/- 100` to `+160 +/- 110 km/s` support interaction and likely merging.
    - Bursts increase inferred mass growth by `2.6 +/- 0.5` relative to adding the pre-existing masses. A Milky Way-mass descendant is an abundance-matching inference, not a measured future outcome.
  - **Position:** Resolves a low-mass example of hierarchical assembly and interaction-enhanced growth near the end of reionization.

- [2609.20442](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20442) Xiong Luo: ELUCID. IX. Recovering the Substructures and History of the Coma Cluster
  - **Question:** Can a constrained simulation connect Coma substructure to its assembly history?
  - **Data:**
    - ELUCID constrained hydrodynamic simulations, compared with Coma weak-lensing, galaxy, and MaNGA diffuse-light observations.
  - **Method:**
    - Identify bound stellar systems and ICL with HBT+SKID; compare filaments and BCG kinematics and trace simulated merger histories.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - ICL fractions span `12.2-23.5%` across models. The main north and west intracluster filaments and broad diffuse-light trends are reproduced.
    - Major mergers at `z=0.74` and `0.45`, and a BCG pericenter `~0.64 Gyr` before the matched state, are model predictions. The BCG comparison requires a small timing offset.
  - **Position:** Uses a reconstructed environment to interpret one cluster; agreement does not uniquely determine its unobserved history.

- [2609.19509](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19509) Anish S. Aradhey: The extreme Disturbed Galaxy Cluster Sample (eDGeS): Spectroscopy and Dynamical Analysis of the eROSITA-Selected Merging Galaxy Cluster Candidate 1eRASS J025859.4-193727
  - **Question:** Can projected structure reveal a merger when line-of-sight velocities appear simple?
  - **Data:**
    - eROSITA-selected 1eRASS J025859.4-193727 and Gemini/GMOS spectra: `76` redshifts, including `51` members at `z~0.2674`.
  - **Method:**
    - Combine projected substructure, velocity dispersion, phase space, and a two-body Monte Carlo merger model.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Three projected subclusters coexist without clear line-of-sight substructure. Dispersion is about `808 km/s`, giving `M200~4.47x10^14 Msun`.
    - The preferred sky-plane merger has conditional post-pericenter times near `0.50` or `1.24 Gyr`. Modeling only two of three subclusters and shallow X-ray data limit the interpretation.
  - **Position:** Provides an eDGeS dynamical case study of projection and merger-stage ambiguities.

## Cosmic-web structure and feedback

- [2609.19993](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19993) Ryusei R. Kano: Topological Signatures of AGN Feedback in the Simba Simulations
  - **Question:** Which galaxy populations carry the topological response to AGN feedback?
  - **Data:**
    - Simba simulations at `z=0` with matched initial conditions and varied AGN feedback channels.
  - **Method:**
    - Compare two-point clustering, Betti curves, persistence diagrams, and Wasserstein distances with same-run sampling references.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Satellite loop-like features show the clearest changes at `0.1-0.4 h^-1 Mpc`; the weaker `1-3 h^-1 Mpc` response is significant only for cumulative full-feedback versus no-AGN changes.
    - Most transport cost reflects shifts in feature birth/death scales, not added or removed loops. Quenched populations in massive halos dominate; topology alone does not identify a unique causal mechanism.
  - **Position:** Adds population-sensitive persistent-homology diagnostics to conventional clustering tests of feedback.

- [2609.19295](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19295) Kunyao Zhao: Reconstructing the Projected Dark Matter Field across 0.1-100 Mpc Scales from the SDSS Survey
  - **Question:** Can galaxy stellar-mass maps recover the projected dark-matter environment?
  - **Data:**
    - CAMELS training simulations, independent TNG300 validation, and SDSS galaxies with `Mstar>=10^9 Msun`.
  - **Method:**
    - Train a conditional diffusion model; average `100` realizations and test projected-aperture masses against group catalogs.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The released map covers `90x90 (h^-1 Mpc)^2` at `0.097 h^-1 Mpc` pixels. Simulation tests give halo-mass scatter below `0.2 dex` and relevant pixel-density scatter near `0.3 dex`.
    - The `25 h^-1 Mpc` projection slab blends structures, and redshift-space distortions are not explicitly modeled. Group-catalog comparisons show mass-dependent calibration offsets.
  - **Position:** Provides a simulation-calibrated environmental map; it is not an independent lensing measurement or a three-dimensional reconstruction.

- [2609.19253](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19253) Olivia Curtis: A High-Mass Size Deficit for Void Galaxies in the Complete SDSS DR7
  - **Question:** Do massive void galaxies have different sizes after matching other properties?
  - **Data:**
    - SDSS DR7/NSA galaxies and an identically selected TNG300 comparison, extending to `z<=0.114`.
  - **Method:**
    - Identify voids with VoidFinder; control stellar mass, redshift, color, and underdensity, and compare sizes and close-pair fractions.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Void galaxies above `10^11 h^-1 Msun` are `11 +/- 3%` smaller than matched field galaxies. The deficit grows in deeper underdensities and persists for centrals.
    - Reduced close-pair incidence supports suppressed or delayed outer-envelope growth. A void label alone dilutes the signal if density contrast is not controlled.
  - **Position:** Links environmental structure to late merger growth while leaving suppression versus delay unresolved.

- [2609.19265](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19265) Judah Luberto: A model to mini-quench early galaxies by balancing stellar feedback and gas accretion
  - **Question:** Can stellar feedback interrupt both the existing gas reservoir and fresh accretion in early galaxies?
  - **Data:**
    - Analytic shell model at `z~6`, compared with observed mini-quenched galaxies and plausible galaxy-formation parameters.
  - **Method:**
    - Balance gravity and accretion against supernova, dust-radiation, and Lyalpha forces; vary inflow geometry and follow shell expansion and return.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - For isotropic accretion, stellar feedback can mini-quench systems up to `Mstar~10^10 Msun`, depending strongly on efficiency and the IMF.
    - Some low-mass cases remain quenched for more than `100 Myr`. Filamentary inflow can shorten this, but sufficiently narrow streams cannot be stopped; shell instability is another possible limit.
  - **Position:** Makes the competition between outflows and replenishment explicit; the result is conditional theory, not a measured quenching duty cycle.

## Resolved dust, clumps, and early quasars

- [2609.19298](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19298) Ivana Barisic: MSA-3D: A Diversity of Dust Attenuation Profiles Across the Epoch of Thin Disk Emergence
  - **Question:** How do radial dust variations affect integrated star-formation estimates?
  - **Data:**
    - MSA-3D JWST/NIRSpec slit-stepping maps: `18` main-sequence galaxies at `z~1`, sampled at roughly `1 kpc`.
  - **Method:**
    - Measure spatial Balmer decrements and compare radially summed, galaxy-wide, and central-aperture attenuation-corrected SFRs.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Flat and rising attenuation profiles are common alongside central peaks. Mean SFR offsets stay within `0.05 dex`, although aperture comparisons have `0.12-0.19 dex` scatter.
    - The median stellar-to-nebular reddening ratio is `0.88` (interquartile range `0.51-0.96`). Balmer lines can still miss regions optically thick to Halpha.
  - **Position:** Shows why acceptable ensemble SFRs do not guarantee reliable internal growth or quenching maps.

- [2609.20079](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20079) Jurgen J. Popp: Physical properties of star-forming clumps in nearby galaxies from CLAUDS and HSC-SSP
  - **Question:** Are nearby star-forming clumps plausible analogs of giant high-redshift clumps?
  - **Data:**
    - CLAUDS and HSC-SSP: `711,307` detected clumps in `211,247` galaxies at `z<=0.32`.
  - **Method:**
    - Use Faster R-CNN detection, injection-recovery validation, multiband photometry, and Bayesian stellar-population fits.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Young clumps have a mass-function slope near `-1.98` in the manuscript convention, above completeness near `10^8 Msun`.
    - Clump mass and age decline outward, with gradients robust to several modeling choices. This supports inward migration but does not directly measure trajectories or uniquely establish the formation mechanism.
  - **Position:** Builds a large nearby comparison sample for disk-instability and clump-migration models.

- [2609.19459](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19459) Anniek J. Gloudemans: Extreme Enrichment at Cosmic Dawn: Three FeII-Strong Quasars at z > 6 from the JWST Aether survey
  - **Question:** What do extreme optical Fe II emitters reveal about early quasars and their surroundings?
  - **Data:**
    - JWST Aether NIRSpec IFU spectra of three quasars at `z>6`, with complementary ground-based and radio observations.
  - **Method:**
    - Fit Fe II templates and Balmer lines, estimate virial masses, and inspect resolved emission and radio morphology.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Fe II/Hbeta equivalent-width ratios span about `2.5-3.6`, depending on template. J0320-3521 permits `lambda_Edd~0.5-6`, with large virial-calibration uncertainty.
    - Two quasars have nearby emission features consistent with companions or ionized gas. Their origin is not secured, and no alignment with radio jets is established.
  - **Position:** Extends quasar-main-sequence comparisons to cosmic dawn; strong Fe II is not a direct standalone abundance measurement.

## Black-hole growth hypotheses and time-domain inference

- [2609.20022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20022) Yu Rong: Rapid Dark Growth of Seed Black Holes in Self-Interacting Dark Matter
  - **Question:** Can gas-induced SIDM contraction rapidly feed a pre-existing black-hole seed?
  - **Data:**
    - Spherical SIDM fluid models in `z=8` halos with `10^8-10^11 Msun`, prescribed contracting baryonic potentials, and seed masses `10^2-10^4 Msun`.
  - **Method:**
    - Solve conservation equations with self-gravity, heat transport, and an absorbing black-hole sink while varying nuclear and SIDM parameters.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Some models grow seeds by orders of magnitude within less than `1 Myr`. Finite reservoirs end the efficient collisional phase well below the ideal unlimited-supply endpoint.
    - Gas concentration is prescribed, not evolved hydrodynamically. The collisional endpoint is not a final black-hole mass; slower growth can continue.
  - **Position:** Develops a conditional alternative growth channel whose nuclear geometry and dark-matter assumptions require testing.

- [2609.20021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20021) Yu Rong: Little Red Dots as a Transient Phase of Self-Interacting Dark Matter Assisted Black Hole Growth
  - **Question:** Could an obscured SIDM-assisted growth episode produce an LRD-like spectrum?
  - **Data:**
    - Gas-triggered SIDM accretion and thick-disk reprocessing models, illustrated with two RUBIES LRD spectra.
  - **Method:**
    - Connect baryonic compression, dark accretion, disk columns, orientation, and approximate Balmer-active spectral reprocessing.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - A fiducial model reaches `MBH~9x10^7 Msun` in `0.32 Myr` at SIDM cross section per mass `1 cm^2/g`.
    - The spectra are illustrative fits rather than full radiative-transfer solutions. Predicted links to nuclear inflows and disturbed environments are tests of the scenario, not evidence that LRDs establish SIDM.
  - **Position:** Connects a proposed dark-growth mechanism to observables; this is complementary to the dynamical calculation in [2609.20022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20022) Yu Rong.

- [2609.19910](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19910) A. Akylas: The rise and decline of a transient obscuration event in a moderately distant type-I quasar
  - **Question:** What changes during a decades-long quasar obscuration event?
  - **Data:**
    - XMM-Newton, NuSTAR, Swift/XRT, SDSS, and DESI observations of a type-I quasar at `z=0.35` over `22 years`.
  - **Method:**
    - Jointly fit multi-epoch X-ray absorption and intrinsic continua; compare optical emission-line changes.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - An absorber with `NH~5x10^22 cm^-2` and `log(xi)~1.4` persisted for nearly `18 years` before weakening, while intrinsic hard-X-ray emission stayed stable.
    - A sub-parsec to parsec ionized outflow is favored, but simple thermal-wind models predict higher ionization and lower columns than observed. Optical-line changes are subtle supporting evidence.
  - **Position:** Separates line-of-sight obscuration from intrinsic variability and exposes limits of thermal-wind interpretations.

- [2609.19672](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19672) Yan-Rong Li: On the Probability Distribution and Null-hypothesis Testing of Cross-correlation for Light Curves in Active Galactic Nuclei
  - **Question:** How can red-noise light-curve correlations receive an appropriate null test?
  - **Data:**
    - Monte Carlo irregularly sampled AGN light curves and reverberation-mapping examples from Mrk 335 and Fairall 9.
  - **Method:**
    - Estimate null cross-correlation variance from autocorrelation functions; approximate the peak distribution and compare simulations.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The fast procedure is implemented in PyAT. It distinguishes correlation significance from lag uncertainty.
    - Autocorrelation recovery remains unreliable when monitoring duration approaches the variability timescale. Stationarity and fitted autocorrelation assumptions constrain applicability.
  - **Position:** Complements flux-randomization and random-subset lag errors with an explicit stochastic null model.
