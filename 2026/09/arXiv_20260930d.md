## Key Findings

- Gas and galaxy tracers can peak away from luminous central objects. [2609.37445](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37445) Sofia G. Gallego locates Lyalpha beside tidal light; [2609.35987](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35987) Siwei Zou finds the strongest compact companion excess in merging dusty systems. Neither result uniquely establishes a feedback or evolutionary sequence.
- The two JADES studies concern one galaxy, not two independent sightlines. [2609.38080](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38080) Jakob M. Helton associates several transmission windows with overdensities; [2609.36656](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36656) Minghao Yue finds no significant excess in a narrower, flux-limited photo-z test. Different selections and unresolved transmission structure prevent a definitive topology conclusion.
- Higher-order spatial statistics may recover information missed by conventional correlations. [2609.36092](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36092) Michael M. Wyatt combines connectedness, pairs and voids; [2609.35957](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35957) Guochao Sun correlates galaxies with squared filtered 21-cm emission. Both provide conditional forecasts, not measured reionization constraints.
- Pop III candidates require a distinction between plausible formation channels and spectral confirmation. [2609.35941](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35941) Eli Visbal predicts delayed pristine bursts near a massive analog, while [2609.35945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35945) J. Scholtz finds that LAP1 still permits both Pop II and Pop III enrichment.
- Integrated light is not a unique physical diagnostic. [2609.36124](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36124) Harley Katz produces very blue UV slopes with negligible escape through delayed nebular emission; [2609.35996](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35996) Naadiyah Jagga finds population-dependent stellar-mass offsets from unresolved mixtures. Neither UV color nor integrated mass supports a universal correction.

## Gas, galaxies, and displaced environmental tracers

- [2609.37445](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37445) Sofia G. Gallego et al. `Caught in the act: diffuse stellar and Lyalpha emission around a halo-less quasar in the assembling cluster CARLA J0800+4029 at z=2`
  - **Question:** Where is the visible circumgalactic gas in an assembling quasar-hosting cluster?
  - **Data:**
    - KCWI spectroscopy and HST/F140W imaging of CARLA J0800+4029 at `z=1.986 +/- 0.014`.
  - **Method:**
    - Identify connected Lyalpha structures with adaptive smoothing; measure flux in the raw cube and compare a halo-matched nuclear limit with diffuse stellar light.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The offset complex has `L_Lyalpha=1.42e43 erg/s`; a quasar-centered halo of matching area and spectral support has a `3-sigma` limit of `4.20e42 erg/s`.
    - Its diffuse F140W-light fraction is `18.8%` for the tidal region, not the entire cluster. Feedback, geometry, dust, and resonant transfer remain degenerate.
  - **Position:** A resolved example of gas and tidal stellar structure displaced from the active nucleus.

- [2609.35987](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35987) Siwei Zou et al. `COSMOS-3D: Diverse Environments and Hot-dust Signatures among Dusty Star-forming Galaxies at z = 4.9-7.2`
  - **Question:** Do dusty galaxies, mergers, and hot-dust sources identify the same high-redshift density peaks?
  - **Data:**
    - COSMOS-3D grism spectra, MIRI imaging, heterogeneous ALMA samples, and COSMOS-Web photometric redshift PDFs for 18 systems at `z=4.93-7.20`.
  - **Method:**
    - Combine spectroscopic counts with PDF-weighted photometric neighbors after removing duplicate matches; compare morphology-selected subsets.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - At `R=0.5 pMpc`, four merging DSFGs have median `delta_spec+phot=4.70 (+1.61/-1.51)`; the full sample has `0.92 (+0.06/-0.16)`.
    - Five MIRI-detected systems lack a comparable compact companion excess. Hot-dust candidates and merger-rich nodes need not coincide; an evolutionary sequence is not established.
  - **Position:** Connects dusty-galaxy selection to the scale and tracer dependence of protocluster maps.


## A transparent reionization sightline: two analyses of one galaxy

- [2609.38080](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38080) Jakob M. Helton et al. `Discovery of the Lyman-alpha Forest in a Star-Forming Galaxy at z approximately 7.57: Implications for the Timing and Topology of Cosmic Reionization`
  - **Question:** Can flux blueward of Lyalpha survive along a galaxy sightline during reionization?
  - **Data:**
    - JADES NIRSpec PRISM/grating spectra and NIRCam imaging of JADES-GS-z7-LAF at `z=7.567`.
  - **Method:**
    - Compare spectral and imaging centroids; test line-spread-function leakage and foreground contamination; model nebular emission and inspect neighboring galaxies.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Blueward flux is detected at approximately `7 sigma` spectroscopically and `10 sigma` in F090W, whose band samples non-ionizing rest-frame wavelengths.
    - Several transmission windows coincide with galaxy overdensities, but a continuous transparent region is unresolved. A foreground interloper is disfavored, not formally excluded; LyC escape remains unmeasured.
  - **Position:** Extends Lyalpha-forest transmission studies to a luminous star-forming galaxy; the companion analysis below uses the same object.

- [2609.36656](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36656) Minghao Yue et al. `An Ionized Superstructure at Cosmic Dawn Revealed by a Foundation Model for Astrophysical Research`
  - **Question:** How unusual is the transparent sightline toward JADES-GS-217704?
  - **Data:**
    - JADES imaging and spectroscopy of the same `z=7.567` galaxy; an outlier search within 100 spectroscopically confirmed `z>7` galaxies.
  - **Method:**
    - Use foundation-model embeddings for discovery, then fit the transmission profile and intrinsic continuum; compare optical depth with simulated skewers and a depth-limited photo-z sample.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - A Gaussian-profile interpretation gives a line-of-sight extent of `210.5 (+55.7/-53.5) cMpc` at `z=6.80 +/- 0.07`, with `tau_eff=1.28 +/- 0.18`.
    - No significant overdensity is established in its flux-limited photo-z test. Supplementary simulations give experiment-level probability up to approximately `0.005`, qualifying the stronger headline tension.
  - **Position:** An independent analysis of the Helton sightline, not a second detection; environmental selections differ.


## Reionization topology and survey forecasts

- [2609.36092](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36092) Michael M. Wyatt et al. `Constraining Reionization with Persistent Homology: a Novel Summary Statistic for Wide-Field Lyman-alpha Emitter Observations`
  - **Question:** Does the connectedness of LAE positions add information beyond pair counts and voids?
  - **Data:**
    - 21cmFAST realizations with mock SILVERRUSH-like LAE observations at `z=5.7` and `6.6`.
  - **Method:**
    - Compare Betti-0 curves, angular correlation functions, and void probabilities using their joint covariance and scalar Fisher information.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Combining all three statistics approximately halves the minimum uncertainty relative to the angular correlation alone: `sigma(x_HI)~0.03` at `z=5.7, x_HI=0.05` and `~0.07` at `z=6.6, x_HI=0.20`.
    - These are forecast lower bounds dominated by cosmic variance under fixed astrophysics, not measured neutral fractions; full astrophysical marginalization remains outstanding.
  - **Position:** Adds topological information to conventional LAE-clustering inference.

- [2609.35957](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35957) Guochao Sun et al. `LIMFAST. V. Probing Reionization with the Synergy Between 21 cm and Photometric Galaxy Surveys`
  - **Question:** Can photometric galaxies retain cross-correlation information after 21-cm foreground filtering?
  - **Data:**
    - LIMFAST mock galaxy and 21-cm fields over `6<z<10`, with Roman-like photometry and SKA-Low instrumental filtering.
  - **Method:**
    - Filter and square the 21-cm field before correlating with galaxies; include photo-z smoothing, noise, and a Fisher forecast.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Mode coupling yields a measurable higher-order statistic despite the loss of overlapping radial modes in the ordinary cross-spectrum.
    - The sign tracks enhanced or suppressed local 21-cm variance near galaxies; it cannot distinguish global emission from absorption. Under the baseline assumptions, increasing shared area helps more than further integration after thermal noise becomes subdominant.
  - **Position:** A practical galaxy-gas statistic for mismatched radial resolution.


## First stellar populations and intermittent star formation

- [2609.35941](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35941) Eli Visbal et al. `Massive Population III Galaxies Near a GN-z11 Analog: The Role of the Baryon-Dark Matter Streaming Velocity`
  - **Question:** Can pristine star formation survive near a rapidly assembling massive galaxy?
  - **Data:**
    - A dark-matter zoom of one GN-z11 analog coupled to semi-analytic star formation, metal enrichment, and radiative feedback.
  - **Method:**
    - Vary streaming velocity and enrichment prescriptions; track halos that remain pristine until crossing the photoheated collapse threshold.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Streaming motion and photoheating can delay collapse into a late massive Pop III burst; the late population is essentially absent at zero streaming velocity.
    - The velocity-averaged visible abundance is `0.24` per analog near `z~11`, assuming a `3 Myr` luminous lifetime. This is a conditional model expectation, not an observed incidence.
  - **Position:** Provides a formation channel relevant to Hebe-like candidates near GN-z11.

- [2609.35945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35945) J. Scholtz et al. `GA-NIFS view of LAP1: Assembly of Pop III stellar clusters during EoR`
  - **Question:** Does the extremely metal-poor LAP1 system establish Pop III ionizing sources?
  - **Data:**
    - New high-resolution NIRSpec IFS plus archival JWST spectra/imaging and MUSE data for lensed LAP1 at `z~6.6`.
  - **Method:**
    - Fit nebular lines, apply low-metallicity abundance calibrations, stack continuum images, and reassess tentative C IV emission.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Components A and B have `12+log(O/H)=6.8` and `6.5`, with approximately `0.3 dex` calibration systematics.
    - C IV remains only `2.1-2.6 sigma`; the C/O upper limit allows both Pop II and Pop III enrichment. Continuum non-detection supports very young, low-mass clusters but does not confirm Pop III stars.
  - **Position:** Revisits earlier LAP1 interpretations with higher spectral resolution and independent reductions.

- [2609.36124](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36124) Harley Katz et al. `MEGATRON: The Physical Origins of Steep UV Slopes at High Redshift`
  - **Question:** Do extremely blue UV slopes require substantial ionizing-photon escape?
  - **Data:**
    - Four parsec-scale radiation-hydrodynamic realizations with non-equilibrium chemistry and synthetic spectra at `z>=8.5`.
  - **Method:**
    - Compare UV slopes, escape fractions and photon-weighted gas densities; test time-dependent ionization-front and recombination models.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Some galaxies have `beta<-2.8` despite `f_esc<1%`: low-density gas delays nebular continuum relative to young stellar light.
    - Blue slopes therefore do not uniquely measure escape. The mechanism requires appropriate gas conditions; dust is omitted and unresolved birth-cloud physics remains uncertain.
  - **Position:** Identifies a non-equilibrium alternative to interpreting blue galaxies as strong leakers.

- [2609.37188](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37188) David Attard et al. `MEGATRON: Dwarf Galaxy Quenching in the Epoch of Reionization`
  - **Question:** Which observations distinguish feedback-regulated dwarf-galaxy growth?
  - **Data:**
    - Four MEGATRON realizations with identical initial conditions, evaluated at `z=8.5`.
  - **Method:**
    - Classify galaxies using sSFR averaged over `10` and `100 Myr`; compare gas reservoirs, duty cycles and synthetic observability across feedback prescriptions.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Suppressed galaxies can be gas-evacuated or retain cold reservoirs, depending on feedback; low sSFR alone does not identify a unique mechanism.
    - Flux limits favor active phases. Here quenched means suppression over the previous `100 Myr`, not demonstrated permanent shutdown.
  - **Position:** Uses quiescent fractions and gas structure to test models beyond their UV luminosity predictions.


## Dusty galaxies, gas calibration, and stellar masses

- [2609.35992](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35992) Andreas L. Faisst et al. `CHAMPS: The COSMOS High-Redshift ALMA-MIRI Population Survey - A New Census of the Dusty Early Universe`
  - **Question:** What dusty populations become accessible with wide ALMA and JWST overlap?
  - **Data:**
    - CHAMPS maps `0.2 deg^2` at `1.2 mm` with a `1.1 arcsec` beam, aligned with COSMOS-Web NIRCam and MIRI.
  - **Method:**
    - Combine millimeter fluxes with near/mid-IR photometry and SED models to identify obscured high-redshift candidates.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Three especially dusty candidates illustrate the survey's reach into the reionization era; their high redshifts remain photometric.
    - Millimeter data can substantially revise optical-only photometric redshifts. IR luminosity sensitivity depends strongly on assumed dust temperature.
  - **Position:** Survey overview for the companion reduction and catalog paper below.

- [2609.36076](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36076) Felix Martinez III et al. `CHAMPS: Data Reduction and Detection of ~3300 Dusty Galaxies at 1.2 mm`
  - **Question:** Which CHAMPS detections support robust population measurements?
  - **Data:**
    - ALMA mosaic with mean RMS `146 microJy/beam`, plus MIRI priors and ancillary counterpart catalogs.
  - **Method:**
    - Build blind and prior-assisted catalogs; use negative maps and source injection to estimate purity, completeness and flux boosting.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The union contains `3355` candidates; `385` reach `>=5 sigma`, with `376` retained after rejecting nine likely spurious detections.
    - The cumulative `>=3 sigma` MIRI-prior sample has estimated purity only `38.52%`. Published fluxes are not deboosted; the robust sample has median redshift `2.54`, so it is not wholly an EoR census.
  - **Position:** Provides the selection and reliability information needed to use the CHAMPS overview quantitatively.

- [2609.37358](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37358) F. Lopez et al. `The ALPINE-CRISTAL-JWST Survey: Investigating the role of interstellar dust, gas and stars in high-z galaxies at kpc-scales`
  - **Question:** How do resolved dust and gas budgets depend on calibration and outflows?
  - **Data:**
    - Six galaxies at `z~4-5` with ALMA [C II]/continuum and JWST/NIRSpec IFS maps on approximately kiloparsec scales.
  - **Method:**
    - Construct metallicity maps and compare alternative [C II]-to-gas conversions with resolved gas, dust and stellar scaling relations.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The adopted atomic-gas calibration yields surface densities inferred to be too high by `1-2 dex`; a spatially calibrated total-gas conversion better matches the star-formation relation.
    - Outflow-signature systems tend toward lower dust-to-stellar ratios and gas fractions, consistent with removal but not a direct causal measurement.
  - **Position:** Links resolved high-redshift feedback signatures to the systematics of inferred baryon budgets.

- [2609.35996](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35996) Naadiyah Jagga et al. `A Systematic Assessment of Total Stellar Mass Measurements with Spatially Resolved Photometry and Medium Bands at 1 < z < 9: The Impact of Mass-to-Light Variations and Outshining`
  - **Question:** Which galaxies have stellar masses biased by integrated photometry?
  - **Data:**
    - 2980 HST/JWST galaxies at `1<z_phot<9`, selected with F444W `S/N>50`; only 153 lie above `z_phot=4.5`.
  - **Method:**
    - Apply matched-PSF Voronoi photometry and Dense Basis fitting to integrated and spatially resolved light; repeat with medium bands excluded.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Integrated masses are preferentially underestimated in high-sSFR systems with heterogeneous populations; the effect is not a universal correction for all galaxies.
    - Omitting medium bands produces mass-offset scatter of `0.33 dex` for integrated fits versus `0.15 dex` for resolved fits.
  - **Position:** Quantifies how outshining and filter coverage propagate into galaxy-growth measurements.


## Spectroscopic methods for faint and blended emission

- [2609.37625](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37625) Michele Perna et al. `GA-NIFS: sRMS, a new method for disentangling resolved and unresolved emission in integral field spectroscopy. Application to distant quasars`
  - **Question:** Can aperture-to-aperture spectral variation isolate hidden narrow-line kinematics?
  - **Data:**
    - JWST/NIRSpec IFS of two broad-line quasars at `z~6.5`, supported by realistic mock observations.
  - **Method:**
    - Normalize overlapping nuclear-aperture spectra and measure their spatial RMS; use recovered kinematics to constrain integrated spectral decompositions.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Spatially varying narrow components can be recovered below the PSF scale. Their sRMS amplitudes are not integrated line fluxes.
    - Mock dual-AGN tests reach approximately `200 pc` for a secondary BLR ten times fainter, conditional on the tested configuration; this is not an observed dual-AGN discovery.
  - **Position:** Complements PSF subtraction and supplies constraints for blended AGN/host spectra.

- [2609.36003](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36003) Mason S. Huberty et al. `MOCSS: Multi-Object Coronagraphy for JWST/NIRSpec Slitless Spectroscopy`
  - **Question:** Can nearly open NIRSpec shutters support blind faint-line discovery?
  - **Data:**
    - HUDF observations with NIRSpec G140M/F100LP in two orientations and deep imaging for host matching.
  - **Method:**
    - Open approximately 91% of shutter area while masking bright optical sources; find lines in dispersed images and cross-match orientations.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The survey identifies 22 emitters at `1.395<z<4.537`, including 18 new spectroscopic redshifts.
    - Seven of 42 detected lines remain without secure identities or hosts. The method demonstrates discovery reach, not complete identification of every line.
  - **Position:** An observational complement to dense-shutter modeling and conventional slitless surveys.
