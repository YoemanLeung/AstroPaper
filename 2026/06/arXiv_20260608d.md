## Executive View

The Monday listing is strong for JWST-era high-redshift galaxy work. The read-first paper is [2606.06609](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06609) Keerthi Vasan G.C. It uses deep SPURS JWST spectra of six luminous `z=5-9` galaxies to measure low-ionization covering fractions from `0.2` to `0.9`, diverse low-ionization velocity centroids from `+70` to `-140 km/s`, and mostly blueshifted high-ionization absorption. This is the closest direct match to the profile's reionization-era gas and feedback interests.

The Little Red Dot cluster is the other main priority. [2606.06575](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06575) Fabrizio Gentile tests a quasi-star interpretation for LRDs with Cloudy radiative-transfer models. [2606.06585](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06585) Sunna Withers uses NIRCam medium bands to identify red emission-line galaxies at `4.9<z<8.9`, including compact sources likely missed by classic LRD selections. Together they make Monday a useful continuation of the June 1-5 LRD theme.

For black-hole fueling and AGN physics, [2606.06620](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06620) Julie Hlavacek-Larrondo maps a JWST-resolved circumnuclear disk in NGC 4696 that connects cluster-scale multiphase filaments to sub-kiloparsec accretion. [2606.06802](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06802) Han He interprets the turn-on changing-look AGN SDSS J1430+2303 through radiation-pressure instability. [2606.06595](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06595) Wenkai Li confirms AT 2023mfm as an off-nuclear TDE with a `1.06 kpc` radio offset.

The lower-priority but useful entries cover cosmic chronometers, gas-stellar misalignment, Milky Way molecular structure, faint-satellite metallicities, and low-metallicity dust in Sextans A. [2606.07357](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07357) Yu.N. Eroshenko is speculative but profile-relevant because it tackles the excess of `z>=10` JWST galaxies with a non-standard perturbation spectrum and suppressed small-scale structure.

## Reionization-Era ISM, LRDs, and Ionizing Escape

- [2606.06609](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06609) Keerthi Vasan G.C. et al. `JWST absorption line spectroscopy with SPURS: ISM covering fractions and kinematics in individual galaxies at z=5-9`
  - **Question:** what do deep rest-UV absorption spectra reveal about neutral-gas porosity and outflow kinematics in individual reionization-era galaxies?
  - **Data:**
    - Program: JWST Cycle 4 SPURS.
    - Instrument: JWST grating spectroscopy.
    - Sample: six luminous galaxies in Abell 2744 at `z=5-9`.
    - Diagnostics: low- and high-ionization interstellar metal absorption lines.
  - **Method:**
    - Measure low-ionization absorption depths to infer gas covering fractions.
    - Compare low- and high-ionization velocity centroids and line wings.
    - Identify systemic, blueshifted, and multiphase absorption components.
    - Relate kinematic diversity to stellar mass and specific SFR.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Low-ionization covering fractions span `0.2-0.9`, indicating patchy neutral gas.
    - Low-ionization centroids range from `+70 km/s` to `-140 km/s`.
    - High-ionization absorption is mostly blueshifted, consistent with multiphase outflows.
    - Half the sample has low-ionization centroids close to systemic velocity, especially low-mass high-sSFR systems.
    - Deep JWST spectra can resolve enriched ISM geometry and kinematics during reionization.
  - **Position:** top Monday entry because it directly connects reionization-era galaxies, gas covering fraction, and outflow/inflow ambiguity.

- [2606.06575](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06575) Fabrizio Gentile et al. `The quasi-star model for Little Red Dots: potential and challenges`
  - **Question:** can quasi-star models reproduce the spectral features of JWST Little Red Dots?
  - **Data:**
    - Targets: JWST/NIRSpec spectra of LRDs from the literature.
    - Model engine: Cloudy radiative-transfer calculations.
    - Central source: accreting SMBH with `M_BH~10^5-10^6 M_sun`.
    - Reprocessing layers: `T~5000 K` blackbody component, dense shell, and diffuse clumpy medium.
  - **Method:**
    - Build a quasi-star model with a convective layer around an accreting SMBH.
    - Reprocess the spectrum through dense partially ionized gas.
    - Add host-galaxy UV emission where needed.
    - Fit observed LRD spectra and derive physical parameters.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The model can reproduce the UV-to-NIR continuum shape and Balmer-break-like structure for some LRDs.
    - It can reproduce hydrogen-line luminosities when coupled to host-galaxy UV emission.
    - It does not naturally explain broad helium lines.
    - It may require additional hot-dust components for some objects.
    - LRD interpretation remains degenerate across compact nuclear models.
  - **Position:** high-priority LRD model paper because it connects compact red SEDs to early SMBH seed growth scenarios.

- [2606.06585](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06585) Sunna Withers et al. `A Population of Red Galaxies with Very Strong Emission Lines at z > 5 Revealed by the NIRCam Medium Bands: "Classic" LRDs, Dusty Star-Forming Galaxies, and a Missing Population of LRDs`
  - **Question:** do NIRCam medium bands reveal red strong-line galaxies at `z>5` that classic LRD selections miss?
  - **Data:**
    - Surveys: CANUCS, Technicolor, and JUMPS.
    - Instrument: JWST/NIRCam medium-band imaging.
    - Redshift range: `4.9<z<8.9`.
    - Sample: `26` red emission-line galaxy outliers.
  - **Method:**
    - Select high-equivalent-width Halpha and [OIII]+Hbeta emission-line galaxies.
    - Compare continuum color against emission-line strength.
    - Classify outliers by morphology and LRD color criteria.
    - Identify compact red emission-line galaxies missed by standard LRD cuts.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Blue UV/optical continua correlate with stronger Halpha and [OIII]+Hbeta emission in the broader sample.
    - The `26` red emission-line outliers split into classic LRDs, extended DSFG-like sources, and compact non-classic objects.
    - Compact non-classic objects can fail LRD selection because of faint continua, very strong line contamination, or flatter colors.
    - Medium-band imaging can recover a more complete LRD-like population.
  - **Position:** important selection-function paper for LRD demographics, DSFG contamination, and JWST medium-band survey design.

- [2606.06583](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06583) Stephan R. McCandliss et al. `The short and long of iPhoton science for a boosted Hubble`
  - **Question:** what unique ionizing-photon science would an extended HST lifetime enable?
  - **Data:**
    - Facility: HST with COS and STIS far-UV and near-UV spectroscopy.
    - Science targets: modest-redshift star-forming galaxies used as LyC-leaker analogs.
    - Program concept: deep panchromatic UV spectroscopy.
  - **Method:**
    - Define a white-paper observing strategy for extended HST operations.
    - Emphasize spectral regions hard to replace with future facilities.
    - Connect low-redshift escape-fraction diagnostics to reionization analog work.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - HST UV spectroscopy remains uniquely positioned for ionizing-escape measurements.
    - Multi-orbit access could improve constraints on escaping ionizing spectral shapes.
    - Community-accessible iPhoton data would support reionization-era interpretation.
  - **Position:** infrastructure entry for the profile because low-redshift LyC analogs calibrate reionization escape-fraction diagnostics.

## Black-Hole Fueling, Feedback, and Transients

- [2606.06620](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06620) Julie Hlavacek-Larrondo et al. `JWST reveals how black holes are fed: kiloparsec-scale multiphase filaments feed sub-kiloparsec circumnuclear disks`
  - **Question:** how does filamentary gas in a cluster core connect to black-hole fueling on sub-kiloparsec scales?
  - **Data:**
    - Target: NGC 4696 in the Centaurus cluster.
    - Instrument: JWST/NIRSpec.
    - Field: inner `618 pc x 618 pc`.
    - Resolution: `10 pc`.
    - Context: radio-mode AGN feedback system with X-ray cavities and multiphase filaments.
  - **Method:**
    - Map inner ionized and multiphase gas structure with JWST.
    - Compare morphology and kinematics to larger-scale filamentary gas.
    - Interpret the flow with tailored magnetohydrodynamic simulations.
    - Compare with the analogous structure in NGC 1275.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The Halpha swirl is a rotating multiphase circumnuclear disk.
    - The disk is physically and kinematically connected to larger cluster-core filaments.
    - Simulations reproduce condensation, angular-momentum loss, and disk-mediated accretion.
    - NGC 4696 and NGC 1275 point to a common filament-to-disk fueling route in radio-mode feedback systems.
  - **Position:** strong AGN-feedback paper because it makes the cluster-scale cooling-flow to black-hole-accretion connection spatially explicit.

- [2606.06802](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06802) Han He et al. `Radiation Pressure Instability in the "turn-on" Changing-Look AGN SDSS J1430+2303`
  - **Question:** what drives the multi-wavelength decline after the turn-on event in SDSS J1430+2303?
  - **Data:**
    - Target: changing-look AGN SDSS J1430+2303.
    - Monitoring: optical, UV, and X-ray light curves.
    - Spectral transition: Seyfert `1.9` to `1.2`.
    - Model outputs: black-hole mass, Eddington ratio, and spin constraints.
  - **Method:**
    - Track the optical brightening over four years.
    - Analyze rapid decaying periods in optical, UV, and X-rays.
    - Fit X-ray spectra and broadband SEDs.
    - Interpret the damping through an unstable accretion-disk zone.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Optical flux increased by an order of magnitude.
    - X-ray timing shows a constant break frequency and a hard lag near `10^-4 Hz`.
    - SED fitting gives `M_BH=4.7-19.5x10^7 M_sun`.
    - The inferred Eddington ratio is `L/L_Edd~0.024-0.046`.
    - The decaying episodes are consistent with a shrinking radiation-pressure-unstable zone.
  - **Position:** useful AGN-accretion entry because it links changing-look behavior to disk-instability physics rather than only obscuration.

- [2606.06595](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06595) Wenkai Li et al. `VLA Observations Confirm AT 2023mfm as an Off-nuclear Tidal Disruption Event`
  - **Question:** can high-resolution radio imaging confirm AT 2023mfm as an off-nuclear TDE?
  - **Data:**
    - Target: AT 2023mfm.
    - Instrument: NSF Karl G. Jansky VLA.
    - Band: C band at `6 GHz`.
    - Comparison: ZTF and Pan-STARRS1 transient positions.
  - **Method:**
    - Image the host at high radio resolution.
    - Resolve nuclear and off-nuclear radio components.
    - Compare the radio offset with optical transient positions.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - VLA resolves two radio sources: one nuclear and one off-nuclear.
    - The off-nuclear source is offset by `0.651+-0.036 arcsec`.
    - The physical offset is `1.06+-0.06 kpc`.
    - The offset matches the optical AT 2023mfm position.
    - High-resolution radio imaging can validate off-nuclear TDEs and reveal hidden off-nuclear massive black holes.
  - **Position:** lower-redshift but profile-relevant as a compact black-hole demographics and transient-identification result.

## High-Redshift Galaxy Formation and Survey Methods

- [2606.07357](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07357) Yu.N. Eroshenko et al. `Origin of monolithic high-z galaxies and UV luminosity of mergering high-z galaxies in the cosmological model with non-standard spectrum of density perturbations`
  - **Question:** can a non-standard perturbation spectrum explain abundant JWST `z>=10` galaxies without overproducing reionization?
  - **Data:**
    - Models: density-perturbation spectra with a high-mass bump and small-scale cutoff.
    - Analytic framework: extended Press-Schechter merger-rate distributions.
    - Numerical check: direct `N`-body simulations.
    - Observational target: high-redshift UV luminosity functions.
  - **Method:**
    - Add an excess around the galaxy-scale perturbation mass.
    - Suppress smaller-scale structure through lower primordial amplitude or warm dark matter.
    - Derive and test halo merger rates.
    - Compute UV luminosity functions under merger-triggered starburst assumptions.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - A bump alone shifts reionization too early.
    - A small-scale cutoff can reduce low-mass halos and the ionizing photon budget.
    - The model increases the monolithic-collapse fraction for early galaxies.
    - The first stars form directly in halos with masses `>=10^8 M_sun` in this scenario.
  - **Position:** speculative but worth tracking because it addresses the tension between bright JWST galaxies and reionization timing.

- [2606.07298](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07298) Swaraj Pradhan et al. `Towards Bayesian Photometric Cosmic Chronometers: Application to VIPERS`
  - **Question:** can photometric and spectro-photometric galaxy samples extend the cosmic-chronometer method?
  - **Data:**
    - Survey: VIPERS PDR2.
    - Redshift range: `0.5<=z<=0.8`.
    - Population: massive passive galaxies selected by rest-frame colors and mass.
    - Age proxy: D4000 spectral break.
  - **Method:**
    - Select passive galaxies photometrically.
    - Infer age posteriors from a D4000-age-metallicity SPS grid.
    - Marginalize over metallicity with a Gaussian prior.
    - Convolve age-difference posteriors across redshift bins to estimate `H(z)`.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The method yields `H(z=0.65)=93.68+-28.27(stat.)+-10.67(syst.) km/s/Mpc`.
    - The result is consistent with spectroscopic cosmic chronometers and Planck Lambda-CDM at the same redshift.
    - Large photometric samples may compensate for lower spectral resolution if passive selection and systematics are controlled.
  - **Position:** methods entry relevant to survey-scale inference rather than the core galaxy/IGM science lane.

- [2606.07160](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07160) Catalina Casanueva-Villarreal et al. `Angular momentum evolution in the CIELO simulations. I. Temporal evolution of gas-stellar misalignments and baryonic merger timing`
  - **Question:** what drives transitions between aligned, misaligned, and counter-rotating gas-stellar configurations?
  - **Data:**
    - Simulations: `44` central galaxies from CIELO.
    - Redshift coverage: `z=3.5` to `z=0`.
    - Diagnostic: intrinsic angle between star-forming gas and stellar angular-momentum vectors.
  - **Method:**
    - Define kinematic episodes by gas-stellar angular offsets.
    - Track the origin and orientation of newly accreted gas.
    - Compare transition intervals with merger timing.
    - Test whether mergers or reservoir replacement dominate reorientation.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Nearly `80%` of simulated galaxies are aligned at `z=0`.
    - `86%` experience at least one non-aligned episode.
    - Abrupt transitions occur when accreted gas dominates the pre-existing star-forming reservoir.
    - The median accreted-to-pre-existing gas mass ratio rises from `0.57` to `2.14` during transitions.
    - Misalignment is fundamentally driven by reservoir competition, with mergers acting as conditional triggers.
  - **Position:** useful morphology and gas-accretion context for interpreting angular-momentum signatures in galaxy evolution.

## Local and Low-Metallicity Benchmarks

- [2606.07421](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07421) C. Gavetti et al. `Dust in the very metal-poor galaxy Sextans A with JWST. I: Characterizing the evolved stellar population of Sextans A based on JWST observations and stellar evolution models`
  - **Question:** what evolved stellar populations and dust producers does JWST reveal in the very metal-poor dwarf Sextans A?
  - **Data:**
    - Target: Sextans A.
    - Facility: JWST imaging from `1-21 micron`.
    - Metallicity context: about `1-7% Z_sun`.
    - Models: stellar evolution and dust-formation tracks.
  - **Method:**
    - Compare observed color-magnitude distributions to AGB stellar-evolution tracks.
    - Model progenitor mass, age, metallicity, and dust production.
    - Use NIRCam and MIRI colors to classify dusty evolved stars.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Tracks with `Z=10^-3` match the overall AGB population.
    - More than `90%` of AGB stars show little or no circumstellar dust.
    - Infrared-excess sources are dominated by carbon stars from `1.25-1.5 M_sun` progenitors.
    - The dusty subset formed about `2-3 Gyr` ago.
    - NIRCam `F277W-F444W` can trace dust production rates up to `10^-7 M_sun/yr`.
  - **Position:** local low-metallicity analog entry for interpreting dust and evolved-star contamination in primitive galaxy environments.

- [2606.06610](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06610) S. Taibi et al. `The Pristine Dwarf Galaxy Survey -- VII. The metallicity distributions of 12 Milky Way faint satellites`
  - **Question:** can deep CaHK photometry recover robust metallicity distributions for faint Milky Way satellites?
  - **Data:**
    - Survey: Pristine dwarf galaxy survey.
    - Sample: `12` Milky Way faint satellites.
    - Photometry: narrow-band CaHK plus deep `g` and `r` imaging.
    - Spatial coverage: out to `5-8` half-light radii.
  - **Method:**
    - Derive stellar photometric metallicities.
    - Combine spatial, photometric, astrometric, and spectroscopic membership information.
    - Refine membership with metallicity constraints.
    - Search for gradients and extremely metal-poor candidates.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The analysis identifies `3917` probable member stars.
    - The sample more than doubles previous spectroscopic member counts.
    - It identifies `170` candidate extremely metal-poor stars.
    - Ultra-faint systems scatter around `[Fe/H]~-2.3 dex`.
    - Metallicity gradients appear in massive systems but not within `2.5xR_h` for ultra-faint dwarfs.
  - **Position:** useful near-field benchmark for low-metallicity stellar populations and future spectroscopic target selection.

## Lower-Priority Methods and ISM Entries

- [2606.06590](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06590) Peter J. Barnes maps Milky Way molecular structure with the CODEX meta-survey, combining NANTEN+CHaMP, ThrUMMS, FUGIN, and MWISP data. The key profile-adjacent result is a ragged spiral-arm model plus molecular-layer ripples with amplitudes of `~40 pc` in the fourth quadrant and `~23 pc` in the first quadrant.

- [2606.06594](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06594) Istiak Akib revisits whether globular clusters trace Milky Way-GSE merger epochs. Simulations indicate that GSE-disc globular clusters lose orbital energy during repeated disk passages, while halo-origin and merger-formed clusters better preserve merger signatures.

- [2606.06628](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06628) Seokho Lee uses two-epoch JWST NIRSpec/MIRI spectra of EC 53 to show that CO fundamental and H2O bending-mode absorption weaken by `~2x` during burst phase, likely from continuum dilution rather than absorbing-gas changes.

- [2606.06644](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06644) Rocio Navarro Martinez compares optical and far-infrared star-formation rates in OTELO `z~0.9` galaxies. FIR-derived SFR density is `3x` larger than emission-line-only estimates, reinforcing the importance of obscured star formation in massive dusty systems.

- [2606.07343](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07343) R. D. Taboada analyzes ALMA Band 7 chemistry in `37` massive infrared-quiet molecular cores and finds temperature-linked abundance behavior for simple cyanides and formylium isotopologues.

- [2606.07388](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07388) J. Allande presents an ALMAGAL line-identification template for the chemically rich high-mass core AG318-c9, using ALMA Band 6 LTE modeling and spatially resolved complex-organic-molecule maps.

- [2606.07446](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07446) Javier Reynoso-Cordova computes dark-matter annihilation and decay limits from total-intensity and polarized microwave synchrotron emission in M31, the LMC, Draco, Sculptor, and Coma.

- [2606.06692](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06692) Sahin Torlakcik proposes a SETI target-avoidance model that excludes about half of a `1.74 million`-star Gaia DR3 sample and retains `777,835` high-priority targets. It is low priority for this profile.
