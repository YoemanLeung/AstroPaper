## Executive View

The Thursday listing is led by a cosmic-noon dense-gas detection, two JWST-era high-redshift population papers, a reionization-simulation calibration, and DESI/Via survey infrastructure.

The read-first paper is [2606.19282](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19282) Jianhang Chen. It reports HNC and CN emission in BX610 at `z=2.21`, giving a direct dense-gas measurement in a massive main-sequence galaxy at cosmic noon. The inferred gas has density `(2-4)x10^6 cm^-3` and kinetic temperature `50-80 K`, and the dense-gas emission is concentrated in the central star-forming region.

The best high-redshift compact-source entry is [2606.18342](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18342) Zhiyuan Ji. It combines VLT/MUSE and JWST/NIRCam line maps for LRD-204851 at `z=5.482` and identifies a thin bipolar kpc-scale elongation through the compact optical core. The morphology and Ly-alpha profile support a low-column-density biconical channel linked to the central engine.

The strongest JWST chemical-evolution paper is [2606.18334](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18334) Vadim Rusakov. It builds a sample of `76` nitrogen-enhanced galaxies at `4<z<8.5`, with the enhanced fraction rising from `~3%` at `z~4` to `~18%` at `z~7`. The interpretation is a short-lived post-starburst phase with nitrogen input from Wolf-Rayet stars or later AGB enrichment after feedback-driven outflows.

The main reionization-theory entry is [2606.18382](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18382) Ali Sadain. Lumina and Thesan simulations show that recombination clumping is more universal when parameterized by global ionized fraction than by redshift. A density-only prescription overpredicts instantaneous recombination rates by factors of `1.29` at `z~8` and `1.84` at `z~5`.

Survey and modeling methods are unusually useful. [2606.18581](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18581) T. Hagen tests property-dependent imaging-systematics weights for DESI DR2 ELGs. [2606.18332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18332) The Via Collaboration describes a dual-hemisphere spectroscopic survey that will map stellar streams, dwarf galaxies, CGM Na I absorption, and LSST transients. [2606.18352](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18352) Osase Omoruyi calibrates a TNG-informed semi-analytic model that reproduces TNG gas and metal flows to within `<=30%` out to `z=6`.

## Cosmic Noon Gas and High-Redshift JWST Populations

- [2606.19282](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19282) Jianhang Chen et al. `The first detection of dense gas in a massive main-sequence galaxy at cosmic noon`
  - **Question:** can dense gas be directly detected and physically characterized in a typical massive main-sequence galaxy at `z>2`?
  - **Data:**
    - Target: BX610, a massive main-sequence galaxy at `z=2.21`.
    - Lines: HNC `J=5-4` and CN `N=4-3`.
    - Context: comparison with starburst galaxies and quasar/AGN host galaxies at similar redshifts.
  - **Method:**
    - Decompose blended dense-gas line emission.
    - Measure the HNC/CN line-flux ratio.
    - Use radiative-transfer analysis to infer gas density and temperature.
    - Compare dense-gas line luminosity with local far-IR dense-gas scaling relations.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - HNC and CN emission is concentrated in the galactic center, coincident with intense star formation.
    - The HNC/CN flux ratio is `1.05+-0.23`, similar to starburst galaxies and below quasar/AGN host values.
    - The dense gas has density `(2-4)x10^6 cm^-3` and kinetic temperature `50-80 K`.
    - The dense-gas luminosity follows the local LIRG far-IR dense-gas relation.
  - **Position:** read first because it directly measures the star-forming dense-gas phase in a normal cosmic-noon disk rather than inferring it only from global molecular gas.

- [2606.18342](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18342) Zhiyuan Ji et al. `Compact Core, Extended Reach: A Bipolar kpc-Scale Elongation in a Little Red Dot at z approx 5.5`
  - **Question:** can the central engine in a Little Red Dot affect its host galaxy on resolved kpc scales?
  - **Data:**
    - Target: LRD-204851 in GOODS-S at `z=5.482`.
    - Spectroscopy: VLT/MUSE rest-frame UV integral-field data.
    - Imaging: JWST/NIRCam continuum-subtracted `[O III]`, H-beta, and H-alpha plus `[N II]` maps.
  - **Method:**
    - Compare rest-frame UV continuum with rest-frame optical emission-line morphology.
    - Extract Ly-alpha velocity components from the MUSE cube.
    - Model the integrated Ly-alpha profile with radiative transfer.
    - Interpret the line maps and Ly-alpha profile together.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The source contains a thin bipolar elongated structure through the optical continuum centroid.
    - A bright `[O III]` clump lies `~2 kpc` southeast of the centroid.
    - Ly-alpha has a bright near-systemic red peak and a faint blue peak shifted by `~430 km/s`.
    - The line morphology and Ly-alpha modeling support a biconical low-column-density cavity in a dense neutral envelope.
  - **Position:** high-priority LRD paper because it moves the class from unresolved compact SEDs toward spatially resolved central-engine feedback.

- [2606.18334](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18334) Vadim Rusakov et al. `Diverse Histories and Common Origins of Nitrogen-enhanced JWST Galaxies`
  - **Question:** what physical phase produces nitrogen-enhanced galaxies in early JWST spectra?
  - **Data:**
    - Surveys: public JWST spectroscopic samples.
    - Sample: `76` N/O-enhanced galaxies.
    - Redshift range: `4<z<8.5`.
  - **Method:**
    - Select high-redshift galaxies with elevated N/O.
    - Stack spectra by nitrogen-enhancement and outflow indicators.
    - Compare continuum shapes, Balmer jumps, Wolf-Rayet signatures, and line-wing components.
    - Interpret the timing with chemical-enrichment channels.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The N/O-enhanced fraction rises from `~3%` at `z~4` to `~18%` at `z~7`.
    - The most nitrogen-rich stacks show low-metallicity Wolf-Rayet signatures and young stellar continua.
    - `40%` of the sample shows secondary `[O III]` and H-alpha components consistent with ionized outflows.
    - NOEGs are short-lived post-starburst systems, either within `~10 Myr` of WR enrichment or after `30-40 Myr` when AGB enrichment can contribute.
  - **Position:** important JWST chemical-evolution entry because nitrogen enhancement becomes a timing diagnostic for bursty star formation, feedback, and early enrichment.

- [2606.19090](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19090) Guo-Hong Du et al. `Resolving the Hubble Tension in the Early Dark Energy Framework with JWST and DESI Data`
  - **Question:** can early dark energy fit CMB, DESI BAO, and JWST high-redshift UV luminosity-function data while reducing the Hubble tension?
  - **Data:**
    - CMB: Planck, ACT, and SPT.
    - Large-scale structure: DESI BAO.
    - High-redshift galaxies: JWST ultraviolet luminosity functions.
  - **Method:**
    - Fit the canonical axion early-dark-energy model.
    - Compare against Lambda CDM using CMB, BAO, and JWST constraints.
    - Evaluate Hubble-tension relief and global fit statistics.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The combined CMB+DESI+JWST fit gives `H0=71.58+-1.05 km/s/Mpc`.
    - The Hubble tension is reduced to the `1.0 sigma` level in the reported fit.
    - The model improves the JWST fit and gives `Delta chi^2_tot=-18.26` and `Delta DIC=-11.89` relative to Lambda CDM.
  - **Position:** relevant for high-z galaxy readers because JWST UV luminosity functions are being used as cosmological constraints, not only galaxy-evolution measurements.

## Reionization, LSS, and Survey Infrastructure

- [2606.18382](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18382) Ali Sadain et al. `The Lumina Project: Intergalactic Clumping and Recombination Sinks`
  - **Question:** how should recombination clumping during reionization be modeled across different simulation volumes and resolutions?
  - **Data:**
    - Simulations: Thesan-1 and Thesan-2 in `95.5 cMpc` boxes.
    - Simulation: Lumina in a `500 cMpc` box.
    - Quantities: standard ionized-hydrogen clumping factor and recombination-weighted clumping factor.
  - **Method:**
    - Measure clumping factors and recombination rates consistently across simulations.
    - Parameterize evolution by global ionized fraction instead of redshift.
    - Map recombination budgets in overdensity-temperature phase space.
    - Define a phase-space clumping factor isolating recombination enhancement at fixed density and temperature.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Clumping evolution is approximately universal at the `10-20%` level when expressed as a function of global ionized fraction.
    - Recombination-weighted clumping remains below the standard ionized-hydrogen clumping factor.
    - In Lumina, a density-only prescription overpredicts instantaneous recombinations by factors of `1.29` at `z~8` and `1.84` at `z~5`.
    - The cumulative recombination count is overpredicted by a factor of `1.45` by `z~5`.
  - **Position:** strong reionization methods paper because subgrid recombination prescriptions directly affect ionized-bubble growth and photon-budget inference.

- [2606.18581](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18581) T. Hagen et al. `DESI Data Release 2 ELGs: Property-dependent subsamples, imaging systematics, and clustering`
  - **Question:** do ELG-property-dependent imaging-systematics weights improve DESI DR2 clustering measurements?
  - **Data:**
    - Survey: DESI Data Release 2 emission-line galaxies.
    - Imaging regions: DES and non-DES footprints.
    - Subsamples: ELGs split by position in `g-r` versus `r-z` color space.
  - **Method:**
    - Derive systematic weights with linear regression on ELG subsamples.
    - Compare the property-dependent correction with the fiducial neural-network scheme.
    - Treat the DES footprint separately in catalog production.
    - Test residual spurious clustering in subsamples and in the full sample.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The DES footprint has higher number density but lacks targets with extreme `g-r` and `r-z` colors.
    - ELG redshift distributions differ across color-position subsamples in the DES region.
    - Separate DES-footprint treatment is needed for accurate clustering in some cases.
    - Property-dependent weights further reduce spurious clustering in `~10%` of subsamples, while the fiducial scheme remains optimal for the full sample.
  - **Position:** useful LSS infrastructure paper because ELG selection and imaging systematics propagate directly into environment and clustering measurements.

- [2606.18332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18332) The Via Collaboration `The Via Project: Overview of the Science, Instrument, and Survey`
  - **Question:** what new near-field cosmology and time-domain science will Via enable?
  - **Data:**
    - Facilities: identical fiber-fed spectrographs on the MMT and Magellan/Clay `6.5 m` telescopes.
    - Survey: a five-year dual-hemisphere program beginning in `2027`.
    - Scale: more than `2,000,000` stars and LSST-depth transient spectroscopy.
  - **Method:**
    - Use `576` robotically positioned fibers across a `1 deg` field.
    - Feed Viaspec at `R~15000` over `505-595 nm` and Boombox at `R~1000` over `360-1010 nm`.
    - Target stellar streams, dwarf satellites, CGM absorption, and transients.
    - Use spare fibers for ancillary programs.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Via targets `100 m/s` radial-velocity stability for faint stars down to `G<=21`.
    - Stellar-stream measurements will probe subhalos below `10^7 Msun`.
    - Na I absorption will support first 3D tomographic maps of cold CGM gas.
    - Ancillary programs include Ly-alpha forest work at `z~3-4`.
  - **Position:** high-value survey paper because it connects Milky Way dynamics, dwarf-galaxy formation, CGM tomography, and LSST-era time-domain follow-up.

## Galaxy Formation, AGN Fueling, and Simulations

- [2606.18352](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18352) Osase Omoruyi et al. `TNG SAM: Bridging Hydrodynamical Complexity and Semi-Analytic Efficiency to Model Galaxy Formation`
  - **Question:** can a semi-analytic model calibrated to IllustrisTNG reproduce hydrodynamic gas and metal cycling efficiently?
  - **Data:**
    - Calibration source: IllustrisTNG hydrodynamical simulation.
    - Modeling base: Santa Cruz semi-analytic model.
    - Halo regime: stellar-feedback-dominated TNG galaxies with `10^10 Msun < M200 < 10^12 Msun`.
  - **Method:**
    - Update halo gas reaccretion efficiency.
    - Replace the cold/hot cooling dichotomy with a more flexible cooling model.
    - Treat galactic-scale and halo-scale outflows explicitly.
    - Calibrate star-formation efficiency and metal circulation against TNG.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The TNG SAM reproduces gas and metal flow from galaxy to halo scales.
    - Global galaxy and halo properties are recovered within `<=30%` accuracy out to `z=6`.
    - The model retains SAM efficiency while encoding hydrodynamic baryon-cycle behavior.
  - **Position:** useful theory infrastructure because it offers a route to TNG-calibrated predictions across survey-scale volumes.

- [2606.19285](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19285) James Agostino et al. `Direct Tests of Black Hole Accretion Rate Prescriptions: I. Bondi Accretion at Different Scales`
  - **Question:** does a Bondi-like prescription predict realistic black-hole accretion rates when fed observed parsec-scale gas conditions?
  - **Data:**
    - Target: Seyfert 2 galaxy NGC 1068.
    - Measurements: spatially resolved gas density and kinetic temperature.
    - Scales: pixel-sized radial steps out to `500 pc`.
  - **Method:**
    - Insert measured nuclear gas properties into a Bondi-like accretion model.
    - Compare warm H2 and CO gas contributions.
    - Compare predicted accretion rates with empirical hard-X-ray accretion rates.
    - Repeat the test across apertures comparable to simulation resolutions.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - CO is the dominant mass carrier close to the SMBH relative to warm H2.
    - Cold molecular-gas Bondi estimates overpredict the true accretion rate by up to `14 dex` for `r<=5 pc`.
    - The overprediction remains at least `8 dex` inside apertures up to `500 pc`.
    - Bondi-like prescriptions can strongly overestimate Seyfert 2 SMBH accretion in simulations.
  - **Position:** important AGN-fueling methods paper because subgrid accretion prescriptions set feedback histories in galaxy-formation simulations.

- [2606.19099](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19099) Juan Manuel Pacheco-Arias et al. `IllustrisTNG50 angular momentum maps: tracing the morpho-kinematic evolution of galaxies`
  - **Question:** how does stellar angular-momentum structure evolve in simulated disk galaxies from `z=3.5` to today?
  - **Data:**
    - Simulation: TNG50.
    - Sample: `~8000` stellar disks from the MW/M31 parent sample.
    - Mass and redshift range: `9.5<=log(M*/Msun)<=11.2` and `0<=z<=3.5`.
  - **Method:**
    - Analyze stellar specific-angular-momentum surface-density maps.
    - Measure four morpho-kinematic metrics relative to the Freeman distribution.
    - Classify galaxies into four j-types with a Gaussian mixture model.
    - Relate class evolution to gas fraction and rotational support.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - TNG50 disks show j-irregular, j-spiral, j-ring, and j-bar structures.
    - Mean redshifts evolve from j-irregulars at `z=0.91` to j-bars at `z=0.39`.
    - Gas-rich galaxies preferentially host j-irregular and j-spiral structure.
    - Gas-poor and rotationally supported systems favor j-rings and j-bars.
  - **Position:** useful morphology paper because angular-momentum maps connect stellar dynamics to mass redistribution and secular evolution.

- [2606.18386](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18386) Charlotte Myers et al. `Reconstructing Galactic Gravitational Potentials from Stellar Kinematics with Physics-Informed Neural Networks`
  - **Question:** can physics-informed neural networks reconstruct realistic galactic gravitational potentials while retaining physical consistency?
  - **Data:**
    - Training inputs: acceleration measurements.
    - Tests: analytic halos and cosmological simulations of Milky-Way-like galaxies.
    - Package: GalactoPINNS.
  - **Method:**
    - Embed physical constraints in a neural-network potential model.
    - Treat an analytic model as a structured prior and learn corrections.
    - Add Bayesian neural-network uncertainty estimates.
    - Extend the framework to smooth time dependence.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The method reaches sub-percent acceleration errors in the tested systems.
    - Orbit reconstruction outperforms analytic baselines.
    - The framework captures non-axisymmetric and time-dependent perturbations while preserving interpretability.
  - **Position:** lower-redshift but useful because dynamical-potential reconstruction underpins halo-structure inference in large stellar-kinematic surveys.
