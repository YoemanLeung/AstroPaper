## Executive View

The read-first entry is [2605.21599](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21599) Qiao Duan. JADES DR5 releases homogeneous Prospector stellar-population inference for about `500,000` GOODS-N and GOODS-S sources, with useful posteriors for roughly `350,000` galaxies at `z=1-9`. This is a high-value infrastructure paper for JWST-era stellar masses, star-formation histories, dust, metallicity, and AGN contamination.

The Little Red Dot cluster is strong. [2605.21589](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21589) Shinsuke Takasao proposes a magnetized black-hole-envelope model that can produce broad-line Doppler components and weak X-ray luminosities. [2605.21574](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21574) Xiaojing Lin identifies `27` low-redshift DESI LRDs at `z=0.2-0.9`, giving nearby laboratories for the same spectral phenomenology seen by JWST.

The early-galaxy tension appears through [2605.22161](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22161) Mikage U. Kobayashi, which uses blue-tilted running density perturbations as a cosmological route to massive `z~6.5-9` galaxies. This is more speculative than the observational JADES and LRD entries, but it is directly aligned with the JWST abundance debate.

For LSS and baryon-cycle context, [2605.21554](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21554) Juan Garcia-Bellido treats the cosmic web as an information-bearing tidal-field structure, while [2605.22497](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22497) L. Evangelista uses JWST/MIRI to map warm molecular gas, shocks, and AGN feeding in the inner `170 x 100 pc` of Centaurus A.

## JWST Catalogues, LRDs, and Early-Galaxy Tension

- [2605.21599](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21599) Qiao Duan et al. `JWST Advanced Deep Extragalactic Survey (JADES) Data Release 5: stellar population catalogue for galaxies in GOODS-N and GOODS-S`
  - **Question:** what homogeneous stellar-population catalogue can support statistical JWST studies of galaxy growth across `z=1-9`?
  - **Data:**
    - JWST: NIRCam and MIRI imaging in GOODS-N and GOODS-S.
    - Ancillary data: multi-wavelength imaging and available spectroscopic redshifts.
    - Catalogue scale: about `500,000` sources, with improved constraints for about `350,000` galaxies at `z=1-9`.
  - **Method:**
    - Fit SEDs with Prospector.
    - Include flexible non-parametric star-formation histories, nebular emission, dust attenuation, metallicity, and mid-infrared AGN/dust components.
    - Use an evolving star-forming-main-sequence prior while allowing deviations where the data require them.
    - Validate against spectroscopic redshifts and release posterior summaries.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The catalogue provides stellar masses, SFRs, SFHs, dust attenuation, metallicities, and AGN contribution posteriors.
    - The main-sequence prior reduces unphysical age-dust-metallicity solutions for faint sources.
    - The product is immediately useful for statistical studies of mass buildup, quenching, and recent star formation in deep JWST fields.
  - **Position:** top infrastructure entry because JADES DR5 becomes a common input catalogue for high-redshift galaxy demographics.

- [2605.21589](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21589) Shinsuke Takasao et al. `A Magnetized Black Hole Envelope Model for Little Red Dots`
  - **Question:** can a magnetized black-hole envelope reproduce LRD broad lines and weak X-ray emission?
  - **Data:**
    - Model target: JWST Little Red Dots interpreted as accreting black holes embedded in dense gas.
    - Parameter space: black-hole masses of `10^5-10^7 M_sun` and a range of accretion rates.
  - **Method:**
    - Model spherical free-fall accretion onto a rotating magnetized envelope.
    - Attribute the Doppler component of broad emission lines to plasma clumps co-rotating in the magnetosphere.
    - Add electron-scattering broadening and estimate X-rays from accretion shocks plus a magnetically heated corona.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The model produces Doppler components up to a few thousand `km/s`.
    - Virial black-hole mass estimates can be biased if broad lines arise in the envelope magnetosphere.
    - Predicted X-ray luminosities stay below `~10^41 erg/s`, consistent with current LRD upper limits.
  - **Position:** strong LRD theory entry because it links line widths, envelope structure, and X-ray non-detections in one physical picture.

- [2605.21574](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21574) Xiaojing Lin et al. `(LRDs)^2: The Low-ReDshift Little Red Dots Survey. II. DESI DR1 Sample`
  - **Question:** do nearby objects share the spectral and continuum properties of high-redshift JWST LRDs?
  - **Data:**
    - DESI DR1: systematic low-redshift search.
    - Sample: `27` LRDs at `z=0.2-0.9`, with near-infrared follow-up for `18`.
  - **Method:**
    - Select compact red sources with LRD-like SEDs.
    - Measure UV-optical continua, Balmer emission, Balmer absorption, BPT location, metallicity, outflows, and variability.
    - Compare broad-line luminosities with local type-1 AGN relations.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The low-redshift objects share compact morphology, V-shaped continua, broad Balmer emission, extreme Balmer decrements, and low metallicity.
    - The sample has a number-density lower limit of `7.5 x 10^-10 cMpc^-3`.
    - Ionized [O III] outflows are common, with a reported incidence of `78%`.
    - Local broad-line calibrations do not map cleanly onto LRD black-hole masses.
  - **Position:** important observational control sample because low-redshift LRDs make follow-up physics easier than for JWST-only objects.

- [2605.22161](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22161) Mikage U. Kobayashi et al. `Blue-tilted Runnings and the JWST Early Galaxy Tension`
  - **Question:** can modified primordial power with positive running ease the abundance tension for massive JWST galaxies at `z~6.5-9`?
  - **Data:**
    - Observational target: JWST massive galaxies with `M_*~10^10 M_sun` at `z~6.5-9`.
    - External constraint: joint consistency with CMB observations.
  - **Method:**
    - Introduce a blue-tilted spectrum with positive running.
    - Compare parameter choices against massive-galaxy abundance and CMB constraints.
    - Connect the same power-spectrum behavior to primordial-black-hole scale perturbations.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Running values near `alpha_s~0.2` and `beta_s~0.2` can reduce the tension to the `1 sigma` level in the model setup.
    - The solution is cosmological rather than baryonic and should be weighed against galaxy-formation explanations.
  - **Position:** useful watch item for the JWST early-galaxy problem because it offers a high-impact but model-dependent alternative to dust, burstiness, and SED-systematics explanations.

## Baryons, Gas, and Environment

- [2605.22497](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22497) L. Evangelista et al. `MICONIC: The multiphase circumnuclear region of Centaurus A as seen with JWST/MIRI MRS observations. I. Spectral inventory and properties of the warm molecular disk`
  - **Question:** how do warm molecular gas, shocks, and the jet connect AGN feeding and feedback in Centaurus A?
  - **Data:**
    - JWST/MIRI MRS: central `170 x 100 pc^2` at `0.3-0.7 arcsec`, or `5-12 pc`, resolution.
    - Lines: pure rotational H2 transitions from S(1) to S(8).
  - **Method:**
    - Fit spaxel-level excitation diagrams.
    - Map H2 temperature, column density, ortho-to-para ratio, and kinematics.
    - Compare H2 morphology to CO, PAH, continuum, and neon diagnostics.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Warm H2 dominates the central region where CO is weak or absent.
    - A `20 pc` cavity aligns with the near side of the jet.
    - Warm `100-2000 K` H2 mass is `(5.6+/-1.4) x 10^5 M_sun`.
    - Shock excitation and turbulent dissipation can maintain H2 cooling and help fuel the nucleus without producing a massive outflow.
  - **Position:** local AGN target, but valuable for interpreting molecular gas heating and angular-momentum loss in feedback-regulated nuclei.

- [2605.21592](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21592) Greta Toni et al. `Molecular gas properties of star-forming brightest group galaxies at z ~ 0.3`
  - **Question:** do star-forming brightest group galaxies have enough molecular gas to sustain their activity?
  - **Data:**
    - IRAM 30m: targeted CO observations of three BGGs in COSMOS at `z~0.3`.
    - Ancillary data: multiwavelength SFR estimates and morphology.
  - **Method:**
    - Search for CO(1-0) in each central galaxy.
    - Convert CO detections and upper limits to molecular gas masses.
    - Compare gas reservoirs with total-infrared SFRs.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - One BGG has `M_H2~3 x 10^10 M_sun`.
    - Two non-detections imply `M_H2<~10^10 M_sun`.
    - Depletion times span `<0.5-1.5 Gyr`, suggesting that gas exhaustion can precede final quenching in some group centrals.
  - **Position:** useful low-redshift group-environment context for how cold-gas availability changes before central galaxies become passive.

- [2605.22163](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22163) David C. Flynn et al. `A Unified H i Rotation Curve Database for 129 Local Volume Dwarf and Irregular Galaxies`
  - **Question:** can local dwarf H I rotation curves be standardized into a machine-readable corpus for cross-survey analysis?
  - **Data:**
    - Surveys: LVHIS, VLA-ANGST, LITTLE THINGS, and WALLABY DR2.
    - Sample: `129` Local Volume dwarf and irregular galaxies.
  - **Method:**
    - Standardize kinematic parameters, distances, morphologies, and rotation-curve data.
    - Separate high-quality tilted-ring curves from single-ring or profile-width estimates.
    - Release JSON, JSONL, and CSV products with worked examples.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - `26` galaxies have full multi-point tilted-ring rotation curves.
    - `103` galaxies have single-ring or profile-width estimates.
    - The database is a useful local-dwarf comparison set for kinematics and dark-matter work.
  - **Position:** data-infrastructure entry, less high-z, but relevant to dwarf-galaxy dynamics and dark-matter tests.

## Dark Matter, LSS, and Detection Tools

- [2605.22685](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22685) Bihag Dave et al. `Dwarf Galaxy Constraints on Interacting Fermionic Dark Matter`
  - **Question:** do Local Group dwarf spheroidal kinematics prefer interacting degenerate fermionic dark matter over a non-interacting Fermi gas?
  - **Data:**
    - Sample: eight classical Milky Way dwarf spheroidal galaxies.
    - Observable: line-of-sight stellar velocity-dispersion profiles.
  - **Method:**
    - Solve non-relativistic hydrostatic equations for non-interacting and interacting fermionic dark matter.
    - Use spherical Jeans modeling for stellar kinematics.
    - Fit parameters with MCMC.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Preferred fermion masses lie around `100-300 eV`.
    - Interacting and non-interacting equations of state give broadly similar posteriors with current data.
    - Large deviations from the non-interacting limit are not preferred.
  - **Position:** useful dwarf-scale dark-matter constraint, but lower priority than the JWST and LRD entries for this profile.

- [2605.21554](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21554) Juan Garcia-Bellido et al. `Information Content of the Cosmic Web`
  - **Question:** how much information is carried by the tidal geometry of the cosmic web beyond scalar density?
  - **Data:**
    - Theory: tidal Hessian eigenvalues and cosmic-web morphology classes.
    - Components: clusters, filaments, walls, and voids.
  - **Method:**
    - Use the three eigenvalues of the tidal tensor to classify morphology.
    - Compute Shannon entropy, shear invariants, multifractal dimensions, and redshift evolution.
    - Relate the entropy evolution to the linear growth rate.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Filaments carry a large share of the matter-distribution information.
    - Wall-like configurations maximize tidal-eigenvalue entropy near potential saddle points.
    - The formalism provides a complement to density-only and redshift-space-distortion summaries.
  - **Position:** mathematical LSS entry, worth retaining because it may inform future cosmic-web statistics.

- [2605.21598](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21598) E. Borsato et al. `Advancing the detection of low surface brightness galaxies. I. ATTILA: multi-tAsking deTecTIon tool for Lsb gAlaxies`
  - **Question:** can automated detection recover LSB galaxies and UDG candidates more completely in deep imaging?
  - **Data:**
    - Imaging: VEGAS `g` and `r` bands covering Hydra I and additional fields.
    - Target population: LSB galaxies and UDG candidates.
  - **Method:**
    - Combine tiling, source detection, iterative deblending, surface-brightness profile fitting, and Sersic modeling.
    - Use the early-type galaxy color-magnitude relation for cluster membership.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - ATTILA identifies `24` new UDGs, doubling the known Hydra I UDG population to `48`.
    - It also finds `92` additional LSB galaxies.
    - It recovers more than `80%` of previously known LSB galaxies in real data.
  - **Position:** useful for low-surface-brightness environment work and future cluster-field census completeness.
