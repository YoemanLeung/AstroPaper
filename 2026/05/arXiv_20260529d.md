## Executive View

The read-first paper is [2605.29623](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29623) Yi-Ming Yang. It extends the MAMMOTH-Grism protocluster program from global mass-metallicity work into gas-phase metallicity gradients in star-forming galaxies at cosmic noon. This is directly aligned with the profile: metallicity structure, galaxy growth, and environmental gas supply in massive `z~2-3` overdensities.

[2605.29344](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29344) Sang Hyeok Im is the strongest LAE entry. It uses ODIN-selected Lyalpha emitters at `z=2.4`, `z=3.1`, and `z=4.5` to connect rest-frame optical morphology with star-formation activity. It is useful for interpreting whether LAEs are compact, clumpy, extended, or merger-driven in the same redshift range where LAEs are used as large-scale-structure tracers.

The early-structure paper is [2605.28930](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28930) Callum Witten. It addresses the reported overabundance of apparent Coma-like protocluster candidates at `z>5`, where current samples appear about `~2x` above the present-day abundance and up to `~300x` above some theoretical expectations. The paper matters because it asks whether JWST-era protocluster counts reflect real structure growth, selection effects, projection, or descendant-mass misclassification.

The quasar and ionizing-background theme is unusually strong for a Friday listing. [2605.29949](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29949) Andrew J. Fox proposes deep `S/N>30` HST/COS spectroscopy of about `20` quasars at `0.5<z<1.5` with `20 km/s` resolution. [2605.28948](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28948) Rongmon Bordoloi frames rest-frame EUV access for high-redshift QSOs as a legacy Hubble capability. [2605.30035](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30035) Cody Carr argues for Hubble-era measurements of LyC escape physics.

The LRD and early-black-hole theory entries are [2605.29895](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29895) Tomoya Suzuguchi and [2605.30145](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30145) Marco Galoppo. They add possible transient signatures from star-envelope collisions in Little Red Dots and a direct-collapse route for SMBH seeds from CDM-curvature peaks.

## Protoclusters, LAEs, and Cosmic-Noon Metallicity

- [2605.29623](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29623) Yi-Ming Yang et al. `MAMMOTH-Grism: Gas-phase Metallicity Gradients of Star-forming Galaxies in Protocluster Environments at Cosmic Noon`
  - **Question:** how do gas-phase metallicity gradients in star-forming galaxies respond to protocluster environments at cosmic noon?
  - **Data:**
    - Survey: MAMMOTH-Grism HST/WFC3 slitless spectroscopy in massive protocluster environments.
    - Targets: star-forming galaxies in overdense structures at `z~2-3`.
    - Measurements: nebular emission-line maps used to infer spatially resolved gas-phase metallicity.
  - **Method:**
    - Identify protocluster members from grism emission-line redshifts.
    - Measure radial metallicity gradients from spatially resolved line diagnostics.
    - Compare gradient behavior with global metallicity, stellar mass, and protocluster environment.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The paper extends MAMMOTH-Grism from global chemical scaling relations to radial chemical structure.
    - Protocluster metallicity gradients test whether cold accretion, feedback recycling, or environmental gas supply dominates chemical evolution.
    - The result is directly relevant to mass-metallicity-environment work at cosmic noon.
  - **Position:** top entry because it matches the profile's metallicity-environment and protocluster gas-growth axis.

- [2605.29344](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29344) Sang Hyeok Im et al. `ODIN: Rest-frame Optical Morphologies and Star Formation Activity of Lyalpha Emitters at z=2.4, 3.1, and 4.5`
  - **Question:** what do rest-frame optical morphologies reveal about star formation in LAEs at `z=2.4-4.5`?
  - **Data:**
    - Survey: ODIN-selected Lyalpha emitters.
    - Redshift slices: `z=2.4`, `z=3.1`, and `z=4.5`.
    - Observables: rest-frame optical morphology and star-formation activity.
  - **Method:**
    - Select LAEs in ODIN narrowband redshift slices.
    - Measure optical morphology rather than relying only on rest-frame UV structure.
    - Compare morphology with star-formation indicators across redshift.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The paper tests whether LAEs are compact, clumpy, extended, or merger-like in rest-frame optical light.
    - Rest-frame optical structure gives a cleaner view of stellar mass distribution than Lyalpha or UV morphology alone.
    - The result helps calibrate LAEs as tracers of galaxies and large-scale structure at cosmic noon and beyond.
  - **Position:** high-priority LAE paper because LAE morphology affects how narrowband-selected structures are interpreted.

- [2605.28930](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28930) Callum Witten et al. `Too many protoclusters? Reconciling the overabundance of cluster progenitors within the first billion years of the Universe`
  - **Question:** are apparent `z>5` Coma-like protocluster candidates too abundant for LCDM?
  - **Data:**
    - Observational target: high-redshift cluster-progenitor candidates found in the first billion years.
    - Comparison set: present-day massive-cluster abundance and theoretical LCDM predictions.
    - Reported tension: candidate abundances are about `~2x` above present-day abundance and up to `~300x` above some predicted counts.
  - **Method:**
    - Compare observed candidate abundance with descendant-cluster expectations.
    - Examine whether selection, projection, mass inference, or definition choices drive the apparent excess.
    - Reframe candidate systems in terms of descendant mass and protocluster probability.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The key issue is not simply whether overdensities exist at `z>5`, but whether they are correctly mapped to Coma-like descendants.
    - High-redshift protocluster catalogs need careful treatment of volume, selection, and descendant-mass calibration.
    - The paper is useful for avoiding overinterpretation of single-field JWST overdensities.
  - **Position:** important early-LSS entry because it tests whether the first-billion-year structure census is physically plausible.

## Quasar Absorption, Ionizing Continua, and LyC Escape

- [2605.29949](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29949) Andrew J. Fox et al. `High-S/N Quasar Observations with HST/COS: Deep Fields for Spectroscopy`
  - **Question:** what science becomes possible with deep, high-S/N UV spectra of intermediate-redshift quasars?
  - **Data:**
    - Proposed facility: HST/COS.
    - Target sample: about `20` QSOs at `0.5<z<1.5`.
    - Spectral quality: `S/N>30` and velocity resolution of about `20 km/s`.
  - **Method:**
    - Build a deep-field UV spectroscopic sample of bright background quasars.
    - Use high-S/N absorption-line spectra to study multiphase CGM/IGM gas.
    - Enable community legacy analysis across ions, kinematics, and metallicities.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Deep COS spectra would improve weak-line sensitivity and component decomposition.
    - The proposed sample would serve CGM, IGM, feedback, and baryon-cycle science.
    - The strongest profile connection is quasar-sightline absorption as a gas-mapping tool.
  - **Position:** useful survey-infrastructure white paper because high-quality quasar spectra are a backbone for CGM/IGM work.

- [2605.28948](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28948) Rongmon Bordoloi and J. Michael Shull `Closing the UV Gap: Rest-frame EUV science from high-redshift QSOs as a legacy-defining capability`
  - **Question:** what rest-frame EUV measurements of high-redshift QSOs are enabled by sustained Hubble UV capability?
  - **Data:**
    - Source class: high-redshift QSOs.
    - Spectral regime: rest-frame EUV.
    - Program type: Hubble 2030s white paper.
  - **Method:**
    - Identify the wavelength gap that blocks direct ionizing-continuum constraints.
    - Frame high-redshift QSO spectra as a legacy dataset for AGN continua and IGM studies.
    - Connect rest-frame EUV access to ionizing-background modeling.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Rest-frame EUV spectroscopy would constrain QSO spectral shapes where ionizing photons are produced.
    - The capability would reduce extrapolation in ionizing-background and absorber modeling.
    - The paper is lower immediacy than a data paper but highly relevant to quasar/IGM strategy.
  - **Position:** profile-relevant because QSO continua set interpretation for UV background and absorber work.

- [2605.30035](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30035) Cody Carr et al. `Extending Hubble into the 2030s to Resolve the Physics of LyC Escape`
  - **Question:** what Hubble measurements are needed to resolve the physics of Lyman-continuum escape?
  - **Data:**
    - Program type: STScI Hubble 2030s white paper.
    - Targets: galaxies and environments where LyC escape can be directly constrained.
    - Observables: ionizing radiation, ISM/CGM channels, and UV diagnostics.
  - **Method:**
    - Motivate UV observations that connect escaping photons to gas geometry.
    - Tie LyC leakage to feedback, star formation, and low-column-density sightlines.
    - Frame Hubble as a calibration platform for reionization-era inference.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Low-redshift LyC escape remains the empirical anchor for reionization-source models.
    - Resolved UV data can separate production of ionizing photons from escape through the ISM/CGM.
    - The white paper is useful for interpreting high-z escape-fraction proxies.
  - **Position:** reionization-method entry because escape physics is a major systematic in early-galaxy budgets.

## Early Black Holes, LRDs, and High-Redshift 21-cm Modeling

- [2605.29895](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29895) Tomoya Suzuguchi and Kohei Inayoshi `Transient Signatures of Star-Envelope Collisions in Little Red Dots`
  - **Question:** can collisions between stars and dense envelopes produce observable transient signatures in LRDs?
  - **Data:**
    - Model target: Little Red Dot systems with dense gas or envelope structures.
    - Observable target: transient electromagnetic signatures.
  - **Method:**
    - Model star-envelope interactions in compact high-redshift systems.
    - Estimate transient emission channels and timescales.
    - Connect the model to LRD phenomenology.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - LRDs may host transient signatures if stars interact with dense nuclear envelopes.
    - Time-domain behavior could distinguish envelope-dominated models from static SED interpretations.
    - The paper broadens LRD diagnostics beyond spectra and continuum colors.
  - **Position:** useful LRD theory entry because it gives a testable transient channel.

- [2605.30145](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30145) Marco Galoppo et al. `Supermassive black hole seeds from direct collapse of CDM-curvature peaks`
  - **Question:** can CDM-curvature peaks produce direct-collapse SMBH seeds?
  - **Data:**
    - Theory target: early high-density peaks in CDM initial conditions.
    - Outcome target: massive black-hole seeds.
  - **Method:**
    - Model direct collapse from curvature peaks.
    - Connect peak properties to seed formation conditions.
    - Evaluate implications for early SMBH abundance.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Direct collapse from rare peaks is a possible route to massive early seeds.
    - The scenario is relevant to JWST and quasar constraints on rapid black-hole growth.
    - The paper complements Pop III and halo-driven seed models from earlier in the week.
  - **Position:** relevant early-SMBH theory, but more model-dependent than the observational protocluster and LAE entries.

- [2605.29876](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29876) Colton R. Feathers et al. `Exploring the High-Redshift 21-cm Signal via Self-Consistent Simulations using Artificial Neural Network Emulation`
  - **Question:** can ANN emulation accelerate self-consistent high-redshift 21-cm signal modeling?
  - **Data:**
    - Simulations: high-redshift 21-cm models with coupled galaxy, IGM, and radiation-field evolution.
    - Method target: artificial neural network emulation.
  - **Method:**
    - Train emulators on self-consistent simulation outputs.
    - Predict 21-cm observables across parameter space.
    - Use the emulator to explore models more efficiently than full simulations alone.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - ANN emulation can make high-redshift 21-cm parameter exploration more practical.
    - The approach is relevant to reionization topology and first-galaxy inference.
    - It is a methods entry rather than a direct observational result.
  - **Position:** relevant because 21-cm interpretation depends on fast, self-consistent reionization modeling.

## Lower-Redshift AGN and Morphology Context

- [2605.28921](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28921) G. Mountrichas et al. `The incidence and star-formation properties of X-ray active galactic nuclei across the star-forming main sequence in DESI-eRASS1: the role of host-galaxy morphology`
  - **Question:** how does host morphology affect X-ray AGN incidence across the star-forming main sequence?
  - **Data:**
    - Surveys: DESI and eRASS1.
    - Source class: X-ray active galactic nuclei in star-forming galaxies.
    - Host variable: galaxy morphology.
  - **Method:**
    - Cross-match X-ray AGN with optical galaxy samples.
    - Compare AGN incidence with main-sequence offset.
    - Control or stratify by host morphology.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - Host morphology is treated as a key covariate in AGN incidence and star-formation comparisons.
    - The paper is a lower-redshift analog for separating fueling, structure, and star-formation state.
    - It is useful context for interpreting AGN demographics in JWST-era samples.
  - **Position:** secondary but relevant because morphology can masquerade as or mediate AGN-SFR trends.

- [2605.29014](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29014) Evelyn J. Johnston et al. `A Deep Study of the Spiral Galaxy W2246f`
  - **Question:** what does a deep multi-wavelength study reveal about the spiral galaxy W2246f?
  - **Data:**
    - Target: W2246f.
    - Context: galaxy structure and activity in a system with quasar/AGN-relevant coauthors.
  - **Method:**
    - Combine deep imaging and ancillary observations.
    - Analyze structure, stellar components, and activity signatures.
  - **Conclusion:**
    - The paper is less central than the high-z entries but may be useful as a detailed galaxy-structure case study.
    - Its relevance depends on whether W2246f connects to dusty AGN or merger-driven activity.
  - **Position:** low-priority follow-up unless the full paper shows a direct AGN-feedback or high-z analog angle.
